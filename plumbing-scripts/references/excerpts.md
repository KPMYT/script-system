# Excerpt library: how the winners say each kind of thing (plumbing)

Short lines from winning plumbing and home-repair scripts, grouped by the job the line does. Each one shows its source as channel · video ID · [timestamp] · the video's views against its channel normal · its tag.

**Sources:** 60 transcripts in `studies/plumbing/data/`. All 40 videos of the four real channels (Got2Learn, Silver Cymbal, Roger Wakefield, Arrow Pest Service), the 15 most-viewed hits of our own three channels (Roger the Pipe Guy, Mark Silverbridge, The Vintage Pipefitter) and TheCheapFix's 5 most-viewed. The recent uploads of our channels and of TheCheapFix are not used. Views against normal come from `studies/plumbing/notes/INDEX.md`. Normals differ hugely between channels (about 550 views on Mark, about 270,000 on Arrow), so compare a ratio only with others on the same channel.

**How quotes are given:** verbatim auto-captions, checked against the transcript. The timestamp is the start of the caption line where the quote begins. "..." marks a cut, and [square brackets] mark an obvious caption fix. Got2Learn's captions run without punctuation, so they read as one long line.

**Two tags:**
- **[real]:** a real person's channel. Borrow the voice and the structure: how plain the line is, how short, and where it sits.
- **[structure only]:** an AI-written script (our own hits and TheCheapFix). Borrow how the line is built and where it sits, never its voice or its wording.

**How to use this file:** to learn the range of shapes each job can take, never to copy wording. Every group holds several shapes. Pick a different one from the last script on the same channel (the rotation log tracks it) and write it fresh from the brief, in the persona's voice. Every fact, price, dose and wait in a script comes from the brief, never from a line quoted here. The scanner's copy guard flags any run of 8 or more words shared with this file.

**What to leave behind on the real channels:** they have a face and their own hands on camera. Anything that needs the footage ("right here", "this one", "look at that"), their names, greetings, sign-offs and catchphrases stays with them.

**Problem lines:** a few groups end with one or two lines marked **Problem:**. They show what our rules now ban or what the comments punished. Never use them as a model.
