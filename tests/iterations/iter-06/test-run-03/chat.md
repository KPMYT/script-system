TITLE: 11 Furnace Mistakes HVAC Companies Hope You Keep Making

BRIEF: Eleven homeowner mistakes that lead to service calls, each with the cost and the fix. 1) Running a dirty filter: overheating and limit trips; $5-$15 filter vs a $75-$200 call. 2) Closing vents in unused rooms: high static pressure, overheating. 3) Blocking return grilles with furniture. 4) Never looking at the condensate trap on a high-efficiency furnace: a clog shuts it down; a vinegar flush costs pennies (refill the trap with water afterwards). 5) Letting snow, leaves or nests block the PVC intake and exhaust pipes. 6) Calling for 'no heat' when the furnace switch was turned off or the breaker tripped: check both first. 7) Paying $100-$250 for a flame sensor job when it often only needs cleaning (a $10-$30 part, cleaned with a Scotch-Brite pad or very fine emery cloth). 8) Accepting a replacement quote for an igniter failure: a hot-surface igniter is a $20-$50 part (brand-name up to about $90), about $150-$400 installed. 9) Agreeing to a new furnace on a verbal 'cracked heat exchanger' without photos, a camera inspection or a second opinion (a cracked heat exchanger is a real CO danger; insist on seeing the proof, but if a tech red-tags the furnace or your CO alarm goes off, don't run it while you wait for the second opinion). 10) Thermostat fan left ON all season. 11) Storing boxes, paint and laundry against the furnace. The adversary is the business model that profits from avoidable calls, not every technician; recommend a yearly inspection by someone you trust. Prices as rounded ranges.
