## The new hook (161 words)

You may be wearing the granny cut right now without knowing it. It's that short, round shape set firmly in place, and it adds years before anyone sees your smile.

Think of the most polished women you've seen over sixty. Not one of them wears that shape. It isn't that they're younger, or born with thicker hair. They've simply learned where a cut sends the eye.

Each of these seven cuts is one you can ask for at your next appointment, with the hair you already have. And the lift shows up instantly in the mirror, before you've even left the chair.

If your hair has been making you look older, the fault was never yours. It's the shape, and where the weight sits. Perhaps you've caught yourself in a shop window and wondered when your hair stopped looking like you.

So let's start with the cut most women are afraid to try, because it's the one that surprises them most.
