TITLE: AVOID the "Granny" Cut! 7 Haircuts That Make Women Over 60 Look Younger Instantly
CHANNEL: Scarlett Starling
BRIEF (talking points):
- The "granny cut": short, rounded, set or sprayed, flat at the back, a dome on top. The shape ages a face, not the length.
- 1. Textured pixie: lifted crown, piecey ends, clean nape, soft side fringe. Good for thinning or fine hair. Trim every 4 to 6 weeks or it grows into a bowl.
- 2. Modern layered bob: internal layers and soft face-framing instead of one blunt line at the jaw. Jaw length suits oval and heart faces.
- 3. Collarbone lob, a little longer in front: the forward angle lengthens a round or square face. About an inch of angle, no more.
- 4. Soft stacked bob: gentle graduation at the back fixes a flat back of the head; ask for a soft stack, not a sharp wedge.
- 5. Long hair with soft layers starting at the cheekbone: length is fine after 60; one heavy line is the problem. Point-cut ends.
- 6. Soft shag with curtain framing: best on wavy hair, air-dries in minutes; heat protectant on a low setting for fine or gray hair.
- 7. Chin-length bob with a wispy side-swept bang instead of a heavy blunt fringe.
- Safety: sudden shedding or patches are a question for a doctor, not a stylist. Heat protectant before hot tools.
- Book match: chapter "The Hair Hack" (Ways 197 to 207): Way 197 "Choose a Layered Bob Over a Blunt One", Way 198 "Get a Modern Pixie With Texture, Not a Helmet Cut", Way 199 "Keep Length Only If It Has Movement".
- Boundaries: the salon and stylist are never the enemy; no prices (not a budget title); no age for her.
