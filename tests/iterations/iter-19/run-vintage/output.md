That usually runs 350 to 800 dollars, and it lands at the top of that range when the line is long or packed tight.

Your pipe stays the width it was built, and Sunday breakfast never costs you more than the groceries.

The clog comes out instead of getting burned through, and your pipe keeps its walls.

A plug of grounds and grease that has to be dug out of a line can easily turn into a service call of a few hundred dollars.
