# Unit plan template

Fill this in from the research pack before writing any prose. It decides what the script contains and roughly how long it runs. It does not decide wording; the plan holds content, never sentences.

```
UNIT PLAN
Title:
Channel:

PROMISE
- shape this time:                 [pick a different shape from this channel's last script; see excerpts §1]
- the number it carries:
- the viewer's fear or wish it touches:  [tools, wasted wood, looks homemade, out-priced by big stores, side money]
- the transformation they can picture:  [what goes in -> what comes out]
- the spark of curiosity:            [and where the video answers it]
- the winning hook it's modelled on:  [excerpts group 1, number]
- the move into the materials:        [excerpts group 12 shape]

CONTEXT
- none / one sentence about:

MATERIALS
- [item] -> own line? yes/no, because: price / store / selection tip / cheaper option
- batched together:                [the trivial items]

TOOLS
- [tool] -> alternative given? (and which)

CUTS AND STEPS                     [one line per step]
- [step] -> simple / earns more because: mistake / safety / alternative / quality / tricky
- collapsed repeats:

FINISH

MONEY BLOCK
- listings: how many, range, "listed at" or "sold for"
- price opinion
- cost per piece
- what's left after costs and fees
- time estimate
- doubter: the objection -> the proof
- listing angle:                   [optional]
- numbers worth saying:            [only the few the viewer needs: parts cost, price, what's left, one batch line; everything else rounded or left in the pack]

WHERE TO SELL

OPINION MOMENTS                    [the build's choice points (which wood, which hardware, which finish or decoration) where the persona says what they'd pick and why]

SIGNPOSTS                          [each section change -> the shape of its signpost line (done-now-next, named outright, a bridge); no wording repeated]

FLEXIBLE CHOICES THIS TIME         [see the skill's skeleton]
- tools listed separately, or folded into materials?
- where to sell: its own part, or merged into the money block?
- a quick check of the finished piece before the money block?
- context sentence: yes or no?

MID CTA
- goes after:                      [the unit where the first payoff completes]

LENGTH ESTIMATE
- number of units, by type
- rough word range -> runtime at this channel's wpm
- too thin to stand as a video? -> flag it; don't pad

VARIATION CHECK                    [against this channel's last few scripts]
- opening shape:
- adversary:
- swear words planned:
```
