## 5. What's still missing, ranked

**1. Curly quotes under "mechanical 0" (an existing rule broken: workflow §4 and D3; second run in a row).**
- The script carries 122 curly quotes, such as "I’ve watched all fifteen".
- **Rule fix:** make it mechanical, not a writer duty.
  - The scanner straightens quotes on read and prints "quotes straightened: N", or it writes a cleaned file that the delivered script must be printed from.
  - The evidence's Scanner line must paste the scanner's own FIX ON SIGHT total.
  - The main session should also check whether the claude.ai copy path curls the quotes. If it does, the fix belongs in the paste step, not in the writer.

**2. Items 25-57% over the length band (an existing rule broken).**
- Median 163 words; #15 runs 204 and #11 runs 189. The length is mostly extra steps, for example #11's "First shut off the power at the breaker, or turn the gas control to off, then close the cold water going in and open a hot faucet to let the pressure off."
- **Rule fix:**
  - Add an "Items:" field to the evidence block that lists each item's word count.
  - Have the scanner split list scripts on "Number N is" and warn on any item over 140 words.
  - Without a mechanical check, two runs have now ignored the rule.

**3. Not simpler: longer sentences, few short punches (a rule gap).**
- Mean sentence length is 16.0 words against 14.0, and 4.8% of sentences are 6 words or fewer against 19%. The persona's "short hammered declaratives" are nearly gone: there are 9 sentences of 6 words or fewer in 189.
- The emotion rules added a scene, a belief and a "but" to each item, with no guard on length.
- **Rule fix:**
  - Add to "Emotion" rule 3: at least one sentence of 6 words or fewer in every item.
  - Add a scanner warning on any spoken sentence over about 25 words; 17 here run over 27.

**4. Item one at about 0:56, and the fix moved to about 1:35 (an existing rule broken, made easier by a rule tension).**
