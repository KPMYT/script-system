STEPS

* find the access hole: line a large hole in the bottom plate up with a screw hole in the top plate (Rockler)
* cut the base square | clamp, let the blade stop before lifting
* center the bearing on the base, mark the spot under one top-plate screw hole, drill a 3/4 in. access hole through the base (Rockler) | clamp the base, no gloves near the bit
* screw the bottom plate to the base (Rockler step 4) | pilot holes optional in pine
* sand the round 150 -> 180 -> 220, vacuum dust (Watco TDS) | dust mask
* personalized: burn the name before any oil (don't burn sealed or finished wood; glue lines in the burn area are a no-go; pine bleeds resin and burns unevenly, test first; keep pen in stand; ventilate) (woodworkingadvisor; Hank book pp. 161-163 for guide-rail tip, light sanding after burning knocks down char fuzz)
* oil: wipe on with a soft cloth, recoat after 6 hours with a light #400 scuff, 72 hours before normal use, hand wash, no dishwasher; food safe once dry, meets 21 CFR 175.300 (Watco TDS; Highland Woodworking)
* center the base assembly on the underside of the top (measure equal from each corner of the bearing to the edge), drive the top-plate screws through the access hole, turning the top to each hole (Rockler step 6)
* felt pads on the four corners of the base
* check: spin it; lift by the top to make sure all four top screws hold (brief safety fact)

SAFETY POINTS (build-specific)

* crosscut: clamp, let blade stop before lifting; no gloves or loose sleeves near the saw or drill
* dust mask for sanding
* oily rags: linseed-oil based; soak used rags in water and let them dry completely outdoors before disposal (Highland Woodworking, Watco product page) | https://www.highlandwoodworking.com/watcobutcherblockoilpint.aspx
* food contact: Watco butcher block oil food safe once dry (72 h)
* pinch points between the plates; all four top screws tight so the top can't lift off when carried
* wood burner: hot enough to burn skin, keep in stand, ventilate, fire-safe surface (book p. 162-163; woodworkingadvisor) | https://woodworkingadvisor.com/wood-burning-guide-tools-techniques-safety/
