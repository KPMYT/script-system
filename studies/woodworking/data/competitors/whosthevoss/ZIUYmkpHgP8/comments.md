7. (66 likes) Old school graphic designer tip for removing adhesive, there is a product called Bestine that was used a lot back in the days when cut & paste involved actual scissors and glue.  The chemical name for this product is heptane and it's the solvent that used to be in rubber cement before they reformulated it so kids wouldn't huff it.  It is a very light solvent that will dissolve most rubber based adhesives, but won't melt plastic or mess up paper. And it doesn't leave any oily residue like goo-removal type products.  The downside is that while it works like magic, it's  just as toxic as most solvents, so wear gloves and use in a well ventilated area.  You won't find it at a hardware store, you will need to either go to a well stocked art supply store, or order online.
