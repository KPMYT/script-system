# HVAC study, comparison 04: models and voice samples

For the Roger the HVAC Guy pack (`hvac-scripts`, copied from `plumbing-scripts`): which study videos should become model files, which real narrators should give the voice samples, and which plumbing models the HVAC set makes redundant. Suggestions only; nothing in the pack, library or study data was changed.

**Sources.** `studies/hvac/data/` (36 transcripts, meta, comments), `notes/` and `notes/INDEX.md` (views and multiples as of 2026-10-27; multiple = views / channel normal); `pack-diagnosis-2026-10-27/references-and-models.md` §1-2; `drafts/hvac-pack-diagnosis-DRAFT.md` §3.8, §5; `plumbing-scripts/model-file-spec.md`; the installed plumbing `models/INDEX.md` (R17, S13 read in full) and `voice-samples.md`; batch 1 (`niche-research/studies/hvac/ideation/greenlit-log.md`).

**Conventions.** **R** = real human narrator (structure and voice). **S** = AI-voiced or unverified faceless (structure only). Proven = 1M+ views real, 300K+ faceless. Word counts are by script from `transcript.txt` between the times given; wpm = words / duration. Every quote was checked by script against its transcript or comments file; a timestamp is the start of the caption segment where the quote begins.

### 1c. What repeats across all six

**Openers.** Every video opens on a moment the viewer is in: "It's a 100° outside." [0:00] (`K2j`); "You're standing in the filter aisle, and there's a whole wall of them." [0:00] (`_Zg`); "Sometime in the next few weeks, on the first cold night" [0:00] (`fHA`); "It's a Saturday morning and you've decided today is the day" [0:00] (`Udy`). Inside 50 seconds the company gains from it: "hands the air conditioning company the easiest paycheck they'll write all summer" [0:38] (`K2j`); "That trust is the most profitable week in the heating business." [0:18] (`fHA`). Then the promise: "Two of them are probably happening in your house right now." [0:45] (`K2j`); "I saved the biggest for last because it's the one that costs eight to twelve thousand dollars" [0:48] (`qbWN`).

**"25 years", all six, in the first 50 seconds:** "25 years I did this, on the truck and in the office for the same companies that mail you the bill." [0:49] (`K2j`); "for 25 years I was in one of those trucks" [0:29] (`fHA`); the others at [0:25] (`qbWN`), [0:47] (`_Zg`), [0:39] (`dOZ`) and [0:43] (`Udy`). "for the same companies" follows on five of the six; only `Udy` drops it. The collection log lists four, but `_Zg` [0:47] has it too. `qbWN` [0:22], `_Zg` [0:43] and `dOZ` [0:37] give his name in the hook; the other three give it only at the sign-off.

**The plug, four of six (13-21% in).** It always has the same four moves: a "quick" opener, "one plain guide" or "a short guide", raydutton.com, and "no pressure", then back to the item. For example: "Quick thing before we move on. All of this, cooling and heating both, the price math and the exact questions to ask, it's in one plain guide over at raydutton.com. It's there if you want it, no pressure. All right, back to it." [2:01] (`K2j`). It never says the book's title, a price or "e-book". The two oldest videos (`qbWN`, `_Zg`) have no plug in the narration.

**The comment question** is always specific and personal: "Tell me how you have been putting yours away, cover on or cover off, power on or power off, and how old the unit is" [11:19] (`dOZ`); "Tell me in the comments what filter you're running right now and what you paid for it" [14:28] (`_Zg`). "I read every" follows in five videos (`Udy`: "because I read them"). A subscribe ask tied to a saving comes in five: "hit subscribe if this just saved you a service call" [14:41] (`_Zg`).

**The sign-off, all six:** "I'm Ray Dutton. Keep your cool and I'll see you in the next one." (`dOZ` says it without contractions: "I am Ray Dutton. Keep your cool and I will see you in the next one.")
