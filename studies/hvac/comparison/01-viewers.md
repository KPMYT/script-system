# HVAC study, comparison 01: the viewers (from their comments)

For the "The viewer" section of the Roger the HVAC Guy pack (`hvac-scripts`, copied from `plumbing-scripts`). It describes what the comments show. It does not suggest pack wording.

**Source.** Every `comments.md` under `studies/hvac/data/*/*/` (37 videos, collected 2026-10-27): 1,501 unique comments after removing top/newest duplicates. Left out: 14 owner replies and the ARS / American Residential Services comments tagged as likely coordinated promotion (8 entries, 4 unique texts). That leaves **1,483 viewer comments**:
- **Real-person channels (R):** 954 comments on 24 videos.
- **Faceless persona or AI-voiced (F):** 452 on 11 videos (Ray Dutton 274, HVAC Exposed 89, Vent Exposed 46, Lost Survival Knowledge 39, Roger the Pipe Guy 4).
- **grayfurnaceman (FV):** 77 on 2 videos; faceless, human voice (a reading in its meta).

**How to read this.**
- **(hand)** = counted by reading every comment. **(kw)** = keyword count by script; blunt. **(reading)** = my interpretation.
- Quotes are verbatim, spelling kept; likes in brackets as the API gives them; then the video ID. No usernames.

**Limits.** About 40 comments per video, so counts are rough. The top sort favours old comments. The faceless set is mostly one channel (Ray). Technology Connections (jokes, 12K top likes) and WINNI (a wall rebuild, many non-English comments) are counted but rarely quoted.

## The short version

1. **The breakdown moment is the commonest story:** ~31 furnace-out-in-the-cold, ~19 AC-out-in-the-heat, ~22 power-outage and 14 "the company can't come" stories (hand), mostly on real-person repair videos.
2. **Money comes as a quote against a part:** 39 comments; 20 are capacitors.
3. **Unlike plumbing, the trade is named a lot:** ~95 distrust against ~34 defences (hand). The two most-liked trade comments point both ways: a cousin who quit a sales-pressure company [3K]; "We are not all out here to screw you over" [1.6K].
4. **The failed attempt is often the tech's:** ~13 own-DIY failures, ~9 failed paid visits, no failed shelf product.
5. **Safety corrections are among the most-liked comments,** ~55 of ~62 on real-person hands-on videos.
6. **AI remarks: 8 on faceless videos, 2 on real ones;** 4 on one Ray video.
7. **Ray's comment question works:** ~121 of his 274 comments (44%) answer it.
8. **Impatience shows as praise for brevity:** ~6 complaints (0 likes each) against ~25 thanks for getting to the point.
