# HVAC study, comparison 01: the viewers (from their comments)

For the "The viewer" section of the Roger the HVAC Guy pack (`hvac-scripts`, copied from `plumbing-scripts`). It describes what the comments show. It does not suggest pack wording.

**Source.** Every `comments.md` under `studies/hvac/data/*/*/` (37 videos, collected 2026-10-27). That is 1,501 unique comments after removing the duplicates between the top and newest lists. Left out: 14 owner replies and the ARS / American Residential Services comments tagged as likely coordinated promotion (8 entries in the files, 4 unique texts). That leaves **1,483 viewer comments**:
- **Real-person channels (R):** 954 comments on 24 videos.
- **Faceless persona or AI-voiced channels (F):** 452 comments on 11 videos (Ray Dutton 274, HVAC Exposed 89, Vent Exposed 46, Lost Survival Knowledge 39, Roger the Pipe Guy 4).
- **grayfurnaceman (FV):** 77 comments on 2 videos. Faceless, but with a human voice (a reading in its meta).

**How to read this.**
- **(count, hand)** means counted by reading every comment.
- **(count, kw)** means a keyword count by script. These are blunt.
- **(reading)** marks my interpretation.
- Comments are quoted verbatim with spelling kept. Likes are in brackets, as the API gives them, then the video ID. No usernames.
- All 108 quoted comments were matched to their source file, text and likes, by script, and 8 were also re-opened by hand (see the end).

**Limits.**
1. There are about 40 comments per video (the top 20-40 plus the 20 newest), so the counts are rough.
2. The top sort favours old, highly liked comments, so a 2015 video's top comments are years old.
3. The faceless set is mostly one channel (Ray: 274 of 452).
4. Two videos are off the HVAC core: Technology Connections (heat pumps and space heaters, mostly jokes, 12K top likes) and WINNI (a wall-insulation build, many comments not in English). They are counted but rarely quoted.

## The short version

1. **The breakdown moment is the commonest story.** About 31 furnace-out-in-the-cold stories, 19 AC-out-in-the-heat stories, 22 power-outage stories and 14 "the company can't come for days" stories (count, hand). Most sit on the real-person repair videos.
2. **Money comes as a quote against a part.** About 39 comments set a quoted or charged price against what the fix cost. 20 of them are capacitors.
3. **Unlike plumbing, the trade is named a lot.** About 95 distrust comments against about 34 defences (count, hand). But the top-liked trade comments are mixed: a cousin who quit a sales-pressure company [3K], and a one-man owner saying "We are not all out here to screw you over" [1.6K].
4. **The failed attempt here is often the tech's.** About 13 viewers describe their own failed DIY attempt. About 9 describe a paid visit that failed or misdiagnosed. Nobody describes a failed shelf product.
5. **Safety corrections are among the most-liked comments**, and about 55 of roughly 62 are on real-person hands-on videos. Faceless videos drew about 5.
6. **AI remarks: 8 on faceless videos, 2 on real ones.** Four of them are on one Ray video ("This guy sounds like ChatGPT or am I insane" [80]).
7. **Ray's comment question works.** About 121 of his 274 comments (44%) answer it: a thermostat setting, cover on or off, their state, the unit's age.
8. **Impatience shows as praise for brevity, not as complaint.** About 6 complaints (0 likes each) against about 25 thanks for "gets to the point" (top [675]).
