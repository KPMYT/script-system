# 03: Body, safety, money, CTAs and endings

HVAC script study, round 2 analysis, written 2026-10-27 for the Roger the HVAC Guy pack (to be built from `plumbing-scripts`). Covers what happens after the hook: villain lines, emotion voicing, safety, money, promises, CTAs and endings, proof. Models the plumbing study's `07-body-craft.md`, `11-reasons-to-stay.md` and `05-emotions.md`.

**Sources:** all 36 transcripts (Ray Dutton's six, HVAC Exposed's two, Vent Exposed, Lost Survival Knowledge, Roger's own, grayfurnaceman's scam video and four DIY HVAC Guy / How To Home videos read in full; the rest read in part and pattern-searched), all 36 notes files, the 37 comments files, `log.md`, Ray's `COLLECTION-LOG.md`, `niche-research/studies/hvac/labels.md` §1 and `CONCLUSIONS.md` §2 and §6. ## How to read this

- **Tag:** channel, video ID, [m:ss] = the start of the transcript segment where the quote begins (the study's convention). Quotes are auto-captions, verbatim.
- **Count** = counted from the data (by script or by hand; script counts are pattern counts and approximate). **Reading** = my interpretation. Unmarked judgements are readings.
- **Faceless** here = Ray Dutton's Garage, HVAC Exposed, Vent Exposed, Lost Survival Knowledge, Roger the Pipe Guy (no footage of a real person working). The other 13 channels film real hands or faces.
- Comment figures: likes in brackets; about 40-60 comments per video, so comment counts are rough.

## The short version

1. **The villain is the service business, aimed at price and silence, with the motive said out loud on the faceless channels.** Ray names a motive in 5 of 6 videos ("the way the pay is built, it rewards the breakdown") but tempers it in 4 ("There are good, honest techs out there"). HVAC Exposed names motives with no tempering and drew the only "this is a lie" disputes. (Count / reading.)
2. **The store shelf is the most-used second villain:** both $30-filter videos open in the filter aisle; the family's health is cast as the seller's lever.
3. **HVAC has three feelings plumbing lacks:** the clock (the first cold night, 2 a.m., "on the hottest afternoon of the year"), the house that won't reach the number on the dial, and comfort ("a sticky 74 feels warmer than a dry 74").
4. **Safety lines are short and placed at the step** (Ray: 25-35 words), with one full block near the end on two videos (Ray's heat video, Lost Survival). Six videos have no safety line at all (count). The biggest gaps viewers corrected: confirming power is off (1.2K, 1.1K likes), a car or generator's exhaust (the car-generator video says nothing about CO), and spray-foam respirators (2K, 907).
5. **Money is said as ranges against the visit or the quote** on the faceless channels (4-13 money mentions per 1,000 words); on-camera repair channels say almost none (0-1.3) and let viewers bring the quote-vs-part numbers into the comments themselves (1K, 849 likes).
6. **Ray's CTA template:** one 40-55-word book plug 13.5-21% in (4 of 6), never naming the title or price; an end comment ask about the viewer's own numbers + "I read every one"; a subscribe ask tied to being saved; the fixed sign-off. His questions about the viewer's own setting or unit drew the most answers in the study (about 20-30 per video).
7. **Faceless proof is first-person tenure plus checks the viewer can run,** never footage. Ray's "25 years" opens all six; his proof devices are home tests (hand over the unit, two $10 thermometers) and sentences to say to a tech.
