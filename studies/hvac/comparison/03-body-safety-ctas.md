HVAC script study, round 2, 2026-10-27, for the Roger the HVAC Guy pack (to be built from `plumbing-scripts`): villain, emotion, safety, money, promises, CTAs and endings, proof. Method from the plumbing study's `07-body-craft.md`, `11-reasons-to-stay.md`, `05-emotions.md`.

**Sources:** 36 transcripts (Ray Dutton's six, both HVAC Exposed, Vent Exposed, Lost Survival, Roger's own, grayfurnaceman's scam video and four DIY HVAC Guy videos read in full; the rest in part, plus pattern searches), the notes and comments files, `log.md`, Ray's `COLLECTION-LOG.md`, `niche-research/studies/hvac/labels.md` §1, `CONCLUSIONS.md` §2, §6. **Reading it:** tag = channel, video ID, [m:ss] (start of the segment where the quote begins). Quotes are verbatim auto-captions. **Count** = counted by hand, or a pattern count by script (approximate). **Reading** = my interpretation; unmarked judgements are readings. **Faceless** = Ray Dutton's Garage, HVAC Exposed, Vent Exposed, Lost Survival Knowledge, Roger the Pipe Guy. Likes in brackets; 40-60 comments per video, so comment counts are rough.

## The short version

1. **The villain is the service business, aimed at price and silence; the faceless channels also say the motive.** Ray names one in 5 of 6 videos and tempers it in 3 ("There are good, honest techs out there"); HVAC Exposed never tempers and drew the only "this is a lie" comments (count).
2. **The store shelf is the second villain;** both $30-filter videos open in the aisle, the family's health cast as the seller's lever.
3. **Three feelings plumbing has no word for:** the clock (first cold night, 2 a.m., "the hottest afternoon of the year"), the house that won't reach the number on the dial, and comfort as a body feeling.
4. **Safety is one short line inside the step** (Ray: 25-35 words), plus an end block on three videos; six videos have none (count). Most-liked corrections: confirm the power is off (1.2K, 1.1K), exhaust from a car or generator, spray-foam respirators (2K, 907).
5. **Money is ranges set against the visit or the quote** (faceless except Lost Survival: 2.2-13.1 figures per 1,000 words; most on-camera repair videos 0-1.3, pattern count). Viewers bring their own quote-vs-part numbers (1K, 849).
6. **Ray's CTA:** a 40-55-word book plug 13.5-21% in (4 of 6), no title or price; at the end a question about the viewer's own numbers, "I read every one", subscribe "if this saved you a call", a fixed sign-off. Those questions drew the most answers in the study.
7. **Faceless proof = tenure + checks the viewer runs + sentences to say to the tech,** never footage.
