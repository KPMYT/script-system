# Group C price research: the shelf, filters, drafts and the bill, the outage

Researched 2026-10-27 by researcher C. National US figures, for rounded on-screen estimates only. Job list: `jobs.md` group C. Method copied from `studies/plumbing/price-research-2026-10/` (README, A-research.md, D-research.md).

## How to read this

- **Fetched** means the page was opened directly and the figure read from it. These pages opened: Target search pages, Fixr, Homeyou, Inch Calculator, ThePricer, Bob Vila (one page), Retrofoam of Michigan, EngineerFix, HVAC.com, Foam It Green, Kyle Switch Plates, ShopSavvy, Slickdeals and Xtreme Air Services.
- **Via search** means the figure came from the search engine's extract of the page. Home Depot, Lowe's, Walmart, Ace, Staples, Kohl's, Tractor Supply and Distribution International all blocked direct reads: they returned HTTP 403 or 503, or a "Robot or human?" page. These figures are probably right, but nobody has read them off the page. Check one before quoting it on camera.
- **Retail prices are snapshots.** Most search extracts don't show a capture date. "Year" for a retail row means "seen in an October 2026 search" unless a date is given. Third-party marketplace prices (often two to three times the store's own price) are left out of ranges and flagged.
- **Store brand and name brand** are given separately where they differ, following the plumbing guide's rule.
- **Independence:** Angi and HomeAdvisor are one company. ThePricer cites Angi for some figures, and so does Bob Vila. Each of these pairs counts as **one** publisher, and the overlap is flagged in the row where the page says so. Homeyou and Fixr are separate publishers.
- **Re-used rows:** white vinegar, 3% hydrogen peroxide and the small wet-dry vac are taken unchanged from the plumbing guide's section 8 (`plumbing-scripts/references/price-guide.md`, researched and verified 2026-10-21). No search was spent re-checking them, and nothing found here contradicts them. Dish soap is **not** in the plumbing guide (only its bigger section 8 list is), so it was researched new.
- **Searches:** 35 of the 35 allowed were used. WebFetch reads were not counted. Items that still lack two independent sources are marked **thin** or **one retailer**, and nothing is guessed.
- **Briefs' figures** (`niche-research/studies/hvac/ideation/batch1-briefs-2026-10-26.tsv`) were used only as leads. Where this research differs from them, a **Brief check** line says so.
