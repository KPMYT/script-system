# Group B price research: AC and heat pump parts and repairs; replacement quotes

Researched 2026-10-27 by researcher B. National US figures, for rounded on-screen estimates only. Job list: `jobs.md`, group B. Method and format copied from `studies/plumbing/price-research-2026-10/A-research.md`.

## How to read this

- **Fetched** means the page was opened directly and the figure read from it (Fixr, This Old House, PickHVAC, PickComfort, Bob Vila, Modernize, Trane, Carrier, American Standard, EnergySage, Filterbuy, HVAC.com, Inch Calculator, Smart Service, HVACdirect, EPA).
- **Via search** means the figure came from the search engine's extract of the page, because the site blocked direct reading (HomeGuide, HomeAdvisor, Angi, HomeServe, AC Direct and SupplyHouse returned HTTP 403) or the page was not opened. These figures are probably right, but they have not been read off the page itself.
- **Counted as one publisher:** Angi and HomeAdvisor (one company). This Old House says its cost data is "based on estimates from Angi", and Bob Vila attributes its figures to Angi and HomeAdvisor, so where This Old House or Bob Vila only repeats Angi, the three count as **one** source. (This Old House's 3-ton AC page is the exception: it uses its own wholesale-price method.) Trane and American Standard are one company (Trane Technologies). PickHVAC and PickComfort look alike (same "Pick" brand, same table style, no owner named) and are treated as possibly one publisher.
- **Not used as sources:** kurlon.com, risingsunartscentre.org and similar pages that look auto-generated; Enercare (a Canadian company; its prices are probably Canadian dollars); Reddit anecdotes (noted, never counted).
- **Dates:** many pages are titled "2026" but carry older dated bylines. The page's own date is given where it shows one. Pages dated 2023 or earlier are flagged.
- Pro ranges are rounded from the sources. "Typical" means where most sources overlap, not the absolute low or high.
- Web searches used: 31 of the 35 allowed.
