# Group A price research: calling an HVAC tech; furnace parts and repairs

Researched 2026-10-27 (researcher A). National US figures, for rounded on-screen estimates only. Method and format copied from `studies/plumbing/price-research-2026-10/A-research.md`.

## How to read this

- **Fetched** means the page was opened directly and the figure read from it (Trane, Carrier, Fixr, PickHVAC, PickComfort, Filterbuy, Inch Calculator, Bob Vila, This Old House, Modernize, Homeyou, Housecall Pro, HVAC.com, eLocal, FieldPulse, Nicor Gas).
- **Via search** means the figure came from the search engine's extract of the page, because the site blocked direct reading (HomeAdvisor, Angi, HomeGuide, HomeServe, Forbes, Repair Clinic, Home Depot, SupplyHouse and permitmint all returned HTTP 403). These figures are probably right, but they have not been read off the page itself. Check one before quoting it on camera.
- **Counted as one publisher:**
  - **Angi and HomeAdvisor** are one company (Angi Inc.).
  - **Carrier's** furnace tune-up figure footnotes an Angi article, so it is counted with Angi for that row only.
  - **Bob Vila's** furnace tune-up page cites HomeAdvisor and HomeGuide.
  - **Housecall Pro** is a contractor software company writing for contractors. The plumbing round found it draws on HomeGuide and Angi data, so it is treated as possibly overlapping with them.
  - **PickHVAC and PickComfort** have similar names and the same style of cost tables. I could not confirm whether they are related, so they count as at most one and a half publishers: never as the only two.
  - **Repair Clinic** and Home Depot's appliance-parts site (appliancerepair.homedepot.com) show the same catalogue, so they count as one retailer.
- **Manufacturers** (Trane, Carrier) are named on their own pages. Their figures are national estimates, not their dealers' prices.
- **Not used:** several search results came from content farms that copy cost tables onto unrelated domains (selector.kurlon.com, partner.kurlon.com, new.risingsunartscentre.org, educationsurvey.steelcase.com, cloud.motorsport.unibo.it and similar). None of their figures are used here.
- **Fixr disagrees with itself.** Its furnace repair page (Aug 2025) gives low "service call plus part" figures, for example a flame sensor at $68-$127. Its newer HVAC article (Oct 2026) gives $100-$300 for the same job. Both are shown where relevant.
- **Inch Calculator's** furnace page is dated September 2023, so its figures are older than the rest.
- Pro ranges are rounded from the sources. "Typical" means where most sources overlap, not the absolute low or high.
- **Searches used: 27 of 35.** Fetches don't count against the limit.
