## C. The shelf, filters, drafts and the bill, the outage
**Filters:** 1-inch fiberglass · 1-inch pleated MERV 8 (each, in a multipack) · 1-inch MERV 11-13 "allergen" · 4-5 inch media filter (and how long it lasts) · washable / electrostatic · HEPA room purifier · **shelf:** fin comb · foil tape · white vinegar (gallon) · dish soap · 3% hydrogen peroxide · small wet-dry vac · carbon monoxide alarm (battery; plug-in; combination smoke and CO) · thermostat batteries · programmable thermostat and smart thermostat (retail) · space heater (retail) · **drafts and the bill:** door weatherstrip · door sweep · tube of caulk · outlet and switch foam gaskets · window insulation film kit · attic hatch cover or insulation kit · chimney plug or balloon · canned spray foam · two-part DIY spray foam kit · rim joist sealed or insulated by a pro · blown-in attic insulation by a pro · whole-house insulation (in words if it varies too much) · professional energy audit and blower-door test · combustion safety test · **the outage:** pure sine wave inverter, 1,000-1,500 watts · furnace power inlet or single-circuit transfer switch, installed · generator interlock kit, installed · portable generator · standby generator, installed · portable power station (big enough for a furnace)

## D. Facts and safety (not prices)
**Carbon monoxide:** where alarms go (every level, outside each sleeping area: CPSC, NFPA) · how long alarms last (5-10 years; the end-of-life chirp) · exactly what to do when a CO alarm sounds (the steps from CPSC, NFPA, the fire service, in order) · CO deaths a year in the US (CDC) · symptoms (flu-like, everyone in the house) · **a gas leak:** the full steps (the plumbing guide's gas-leak row, re-checked) · **generators and cars:** the distance from doors, windows and vents (CDC, CPSC), never in a garage even with the door open, never backfeed a panel (why: line workers) · **space heaters:** NFPA's share of home heating fires and deaths, the three-foot rule, straight into a wall outlet (CPSC, NFPA, UL) · **filters:** how often to change (1-3 months; monthly with pets) · EPA's MERV advice · **thermostats:** the Department of Energy's setback figure (7-10 degrees for 8 hours, up to 10% a year), the heat pump caveat · **ceiling fans:** winter direction (Department of Energy) · **refrigerant:** R-22 production ended (2020) · the R-410A changes for new systems (AIM Act, 2025) · the newer refrigerants · EPA section 608 certification to buy or handle refrigerant · **furnaces:** how much water a condensing furnace makes in cold weather · why a condensate trap must hold water · typical lifespans (furnace, central AC, heat pump) · how many watts a gas furnace needs to run · **capacitors** hold a charge after the power is off (why) · **air sealing:** ENERGY STAR's savings figure · indoor humidity range (EPA) · **federal tax credits** for heat pumps and furnaces after 2025 (status only; "in words" unless irs.gov is clear)
