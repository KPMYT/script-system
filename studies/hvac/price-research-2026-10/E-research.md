## How to read this

- **Fetched** means the page was opened directly and the figure read from it. These pages opened: Target search pages, Harbor Freight search pages, Elliott Electric (one product page), PartsSource (one product page) and Klein Tools' own product pages (which show no price).
- **Via search** means the figure came from the search engine's extract of the page. Home Depot, Lowe's, Walmart, Ace, O'Reilly and Amazon blocked direct reads (HTTP 403 or a "Robot or human?" page), or the page opened but cut off before the price (Menards, True Value, Do it Best, Amazon). These figures are probably right, but nobody has read them off the page. Check one before quoting it on camera.
- **Retail prices are snapshots.** Most search extracts don't show a capture date. "Oct 2026" for a retail row means "seen in an October 2026 search" unless a date is given. Third-party marketplace listings and bulk or commercial cases are left out of ranges and flagged.
- **Store brand and name brand** are given separately where they differ, following the plumbing guide's rule.
- **Independence:** every source below is a different company. Walmart Business is counted with Walmart, not as its own source.
- **Searches:** 8 of the 8 allowed were used. WebFetch reads were not counted. Nothing is guessed; gaps are listed under "Not found".
- **Briefs' figures:** the batch-1 briefs name the emery cloth and the scrub pad but give no price for either, and don't price a voltage tester. So there is no **Brief check** line in this file.

### Very fine emery cloth (roll or small pack)

| DIY part | Pro range | Average |
|---|---|---|
| 320-grit roll (1 in wide, 10-25 yd) about $14 at big-box and hardware listings; $7.54 at an industrial parts seller. Small assorted sheet packs $5-$6 | n/a | n/a |

What moves the price: grit (emery cloth is sold by grit; "very fine" usually means 320 and up, while 180 is sold as fine), roll length, and roll or sheets. The small assorted packs that hardware stores stock (3M, Gator) top out at about 180 grit. Multi-grit "80 to 600" roll sets from third-party sellers on Home Depot and Lowe's ($58-$104) are left out. Bulk boxes (25-50 sheets, $29-$116 at Walmart) are left out.
