# Group E price check (second, independent checker)

Checked 2026-10-27 against `E-research.md` (three shelf items: a pen-type voltage tester, very fine emery cloth, a green scrub pad pack). National US figures, for rounded on-screen estimates only. Format copied from `C-verify.md`.

## How this check was done

- I counted only sellers the researcher did **not** use for that item. The researcher's sources were: **voltage tester:** Home Depot, Lowe's, Elliott Electric Supply, Menards, Walmart, Harbor Freight, Target, a Christian Post deals page, Klein's own site (no price); **emery cloth:** Home Depot, Walmart, PartsSource, True Value (corporate site), Menards, Harbor Freight, Target; **scrub pads:** Target, Walmart, Home Depot. I left all of those out, plus Amazon and eBay.
- **Read live** means I opened the page and read the figure off it myself. **Via search** means the figure came only from the search engine's summary. Pages that blocked direct reads (HTTP 403 or 405): Zoro, Acme Tools, GME Supply, Test Equipment Depot, O'Reilly, Meijer, ShopRite. Kellogg Supply opened but found no voltage tester. McCoy's opened but said the product was not found.
- **Note on independence:** several of the small hardware stores below (Cottins Hardware, James Trading Co., Beaver Creek Lumber, JW Hardware, Hills Flat Lumber, Denver True Value) are independent stores whose websites run on a shared co-op catalog. Their prices are often the co-op's suggested retail price, so the same figure turning up at three of them counts as one data point, not three. Denver True Value is a True Value member store; the researcher used True Value's corporate site only for emery cloth, not for scrub pads.
- Web searches used: **4 of 6**. WebFetch reads were not counted.
- Retail prices are October 2026 snapshots.

## Flags

- **Klein NCVT1P sells at $19.97 almost everywhere outside the big boxes** (Ohio Power Tool, JB Tools, Specialized.net, GME Supply). That agrees with the researcher's Lowe's $19.98 and the deal site's $19.97 list figure. "About $20" is safe.
- **Southwire's $31 "MSRP"** (DWG) is a list price nobody seems to charge; selling prices found were $12.49-$24.33 (researcher) and $14.00 (Cripe). Don't quote the MSRP.
- **"Very fine" emery cloth on a store shelf:** the researcher found only 180-grit packs in stores. I found 320-grit rolls on small hardware and farm-store listings ($6.99-$13.99, read live), but Cottins lists it as special order, and none of these pages showed it on a shelf in a given store. So the script can say the price with confidence, but shouldn't promise every hardware store stocks the very fine grit.
- **Scrub pad 6-pack:** $5.19 at Runnings is within a cent of the researcher's Walmart $5.18; the $6.79 at Cottins is a special-order price.
