You may have a catch tank beneath the coils and a pipe that drains the catch tank by gravity ONLY!  The water will drip from the coils into the tank and then flow down the pipe. If such is the case, and you have a clog, you generally only need to break up or remove the clog and pour water down the U-Trap's access port to literally flush the clog away.  Just thought I'd mention this because it literally caught ME by surprise with my own HVAC system.
