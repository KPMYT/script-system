1. (29 likes) [names a contractor: ARS / American Residential Services] The installation quality point is the one most people skip over and then regret. A Goodman installed by someone who pulls a proper vacuum, documents the refrigerant charge, and does a Manual J calc will outperform a Trane installed by someone eyeballing everything. The brand debate is mostly downstream of that. One thing worth adding to this: when you get a quote, ask for a written breakdown that separates equipment, labor, and materials. That said, branch-level performance varies, so check local reviews before committing to anyone.

(34 likes) [names a contractor: ARS / American Residential Services] The brand consolidation point is real and worth understanding, but the installation quality section is where most homeowners actually lose money. A properly sized and charged system from a mid-tier manufacturer will outperform a premium unit that was rushed in without a Manual J calc or a proper vacuum pull. The brand on the box matters less than whether the contractor is pulling permits, documenting refrigerant charge, and sizing to your actual load rather than the old unit's tonnage. Before approving any quote, ask for an itemized breakdown separating equipment from labor, and ask what sizing method they used. American Residential Services is one option worth comparing quotes against, though branch-level quality can vary, so check local reviews and ask those same questions before signing anything.
