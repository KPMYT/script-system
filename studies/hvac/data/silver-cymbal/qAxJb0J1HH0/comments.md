The secondary drain line should be completely separated.  Even if you don't wish to install another drain line for the secondary, you can install a Safe-T switch which is a great option. Since your primary and secondary are joined, you have no backup.  Next is your inline trap.  I would recommend replacing that with a P-trap and vent.
