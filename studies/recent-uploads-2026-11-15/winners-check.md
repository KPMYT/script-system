## 3. What this means

1. **Problem 2:** the limit line in the opening is something our winners almost never did. Where they did, they framed it as a promise. In that slot, the winners gave the viewer a reason to stay (a tease, a named numbered item). Our five give a reason some viewers might go ("this isn't for your clog"). That fits stage 2's drop, but it's a reading, not proof.
2. **Problem 3:** the old winners sold the book in general terms, or not at all mid-video. Specific numbers came in October. They held on the two recent winners, where they matched what had just been taught. The one clear loss in our five is a mismatch. So the evidence points to the match and the load (how many numbers and topics), not to having numbers at all.
