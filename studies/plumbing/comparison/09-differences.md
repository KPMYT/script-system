# 09 Differences: every variable, group by group

Plumbing script study, written 2026-10-20. The operator asked for "all differences between scripts in these variables": value, core emotions, what commenters appreciate, hook structure, hook triggers, outro, video structure and the rest. This file puts them side by side in one place. It is built only from `README.md`, `stats.md` and files `01`-`08` in this folder, plus the notes, transcripts and comment files under `studies/plumbing/` where a cell needed filling. Nothing in the library was changed.

## How to read this

- **One section per variable.** Each has a table with one row per group, always in the same order: Roger hits, Roger recent, Mark hits, Mark recent, Vintage hits, Vintage recent, Silver Cymbal, Roger Wakefield, Got2Learn, Arrow Pest, TheCheapFix. Under each table, "The differences that matter" and a one-line **So for our scripts (suggestion)**. Where the operator has already decided something, the line says **Decided** instead.
- **Groups.** "Hits" are a channel's 5 most-viewed in the last 24 months (Got2Learn: all-time, 2017-2021). "Recent" are its 5 newest. Own hits: February-August 2026. Own recent: 12-19 October 2026, on a newer template, 1-8 days old, with 4 real comments between the 15 of them.
- **Format.** FACE: Silver Cymbal, Roger Wakefield, Arrow. FACELESS: the three own channels (AI voice, compiled footage), Got2Learn (his voice over his own hands-on footage) and TheCheapFix (AI voice, compiled footage, the only like-for-like).
- **Tags.** Script quotes: `(video ID [m:ss])`. Timestamps are caption-block starts and can be up to about 30 s early; where two files give slightly different times for the same line, I used one. Comment quotes: `(video ID, likes)`, never usernames.
- **Numbers.** Medians and counts come from the file named in brackets. "x" is views divided by that channel's normal; compare it **within a channel only**. "~" means a hand count from about 40 sampled comments per video. Where files disagree, both figures are given with the method.
- **Read every own hits-vs-recent difference as a thing to test.** The two groups differ in month, template, age and comment count all at once. Samples are 5 videos per group.

## What we can't tell from this data

- **Causes.** Every own hits-vs-recent difference is mixed up with month, template, video age, the algorithm and audience fatigue. These are tests, not proven effects.

- **The footage.** No video was watched; mismatch is known only from comments.
- **How the new template lands.** The 15 own recent uploads have 4 real comments between them.
- **Comments beyond about 40 per video.** Rarer themes (shame, defiance) may be under-counted; most counts are one coder's hand counts.
- **Face vs faceless.** Silver, Wakefield and Arrow can't be separated into script effect and camera effect; TheCheapFix is the only like-for-like, with 10 videos. Got2Learn's hits are 2017-2021; Arrow is pest control.
- **CTA conversion.** Whether the long QR pitch sold more books, or the two-line e-book CTA will capture more emails, isn't in the data.
- **Facts.** Prices, temperatures, TheCheapFix's citations, "since 1874" and the other flagged claims were not fact-checked.
