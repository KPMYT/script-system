# Competitors: what five channels do, and what a faceless plumbing channel can take from them

Study date: 2026-10-20. Covers Silver Cymbal, Roger Wakefield Plumbing Education, Got2Learn, Arrow Pest Service and TheCheapFix: 5 most recent videos ("recent") and 5 most-viewed videos from the last 24 months ("hits") per channel. For Got2Learn the hits are its 5 all-time most-viewed (2017-2021), by the operator's decision.

Sources: `comparison/stats.md`, `notes/INDEX.md`, the 50 competitor notes files, the five competitor transcripts (TheCheapFix and Silver Cymbal read in full; the other three read closely for the first two minutes of every video and skimmed for structure, with the notes used for body detail), every competitor `comments.md` and all five `COLLECTION-LOG.md` files.

How to read this:
- **Measured** means counted or timed from the data. **Inference** means my reading of it. Unlabelled quotes and numbers are measured; anything about cause or intent is inference.
- Quotes are auto-captions, so a few words may be misheard. Timestamps are within a few seconds.
- Comment counts are rough. Each video has about 20 top and 20 newest comments (the comment tool would not page further), so "~8 of 39" means 8 in that sample.

---

## 0. Limits: read this first

1. **Five videos per group.** One odd video moves any median.
2. **Very different normals.** Views/normal divides by each channel's typical recent video: Silver Cymbal 70,989; Roger Wakefield 5,210; Got2Learn 121,799; Arrow 270,890; TheCheapFix 7,670. Roger Wakefield's 143x hit (742k views) has fewer views than Silver Cymbal's 15x hit (1.07M). Compare views across channels, ratios only within one.
3. **Recent videos are young.** All five TheCheapFix recent videos were 1-6 days old at collection, and four had no comments yet. Nothing firm can be said about its recent titles from views.
4. **Got2Learn is another era.** Its hits are 2017-2021 uploads with up to nine years to collect views, set against a normal built from 2021-2025 uploads. The channel has stopped uploading.
5. **Arrow is a different niche** (pest control). Its lessons are about format, not topic.
6. **Not in the data:** thumbnails, click-through rate, retention, traffic sources and the footage itself (only commenters describe it). Any reading of why a title worked leaves out the thumbnail, which may matter as much.
7. **Format labels.** Silver Cymbal, Roger Wakefield and Arrow are FACE. Got2Learn is FACELESS (voice-over over his own hands-on demos; one 2025 update is on camera). TheCheapFix is FACELESS and labelled "AI channel" by the operator; no video was watched to confirm the voice is synthetic.

### 3.8 What transfers, and what to avoid (inference)

**Transfers:** the hit title formula (already working on the operator's own channels); the five-part hook kept under about 50 seconds; count-up items with a fixed inner order (scene, cost, method with quantities and waits, payoff), each under a minute; a "what it won't do" line ("It will not bust a clog", cljD9InmFzk [9:13]); a correction framed as honesty; one safety block plus a concrete call-a-pro trigger ("if water backs up out of two fixtures at once, you have a main line problem, and you need someone with a camera", SUqzcj5udsk [9:42]).

**Avoid:** narration that forbids an item the visuals then show (the most common visible complaint; a script line like "peroxide only, no baking soda" invites a baking-soda clip); claims tradespeople can disprove (coils, rust, unclogging); reused passages and cross-video contradictions; safety counted as list items to pad the number; claimed first-hand experience from a nameless "we".

## 4. Patterns across all five competitors

1. **The promise is paid fast.** First real value at 0:15-0:49 on four channels' hits; Arrow's 1:34 is filled with live footage of the problem. The operator's hits: 1:11-1:34 (measured).
2. **Hooks are short:** 50-145 words on the hits, against 165-281 for the operator (measured).
3. **Explicit counts dominate:** all 10 TheCheapFix videos, 6 of 9 Got2Learn teaching videos, 4 of 5 Roger Wakefield recent videos, Arrow's spoken steps and levels. Spoken labels ("Point number two", "the tenth violation") let a viewer track progress without a face.
4. **Cheap fix vs expensive pro or product,** in every channel. Exact prices draw disputes everywhere ("$500 not 3k"; "Kit is $100 now"; "Its not a dollar anymore").
5. **Adversaries are mostly products, makers, salesmen or insurers.** Only TheCheapFix makes the trade the villain in every video, and its HVAC hit drew ~5 mocking comments from technicians.
6. **An honest limit, said plainly, appears in every channel:** duct seal's limits, "you don't really need to address these", "not legal advice", "I'm not going to oversell this", "It will not bust a clog".
7. **Comments reward brevity and punish padding:** the most-liked comments on Silver Cymbal and Got2Learn hits praise short, no-intro videos; TheCheapFix and Arrow draw "get to the point" and "too long".
8. **Comments check facts everywhere.** On face channels corrections come with goodwill; on TheCheapFix they come with "AI slop".
9. **AI remarks track the format:** 0 / 3 / 2 / 3 / 13 (measured, table in section 1). Inference: "real" is now a selling point face-channel viewers say out loud, and AI channels are judged hardest on voice glitches and mismatched footage, both of which a script can reduce.
10. **Length has no single norm.** Hits run 3:25-6:37 (Silver Cymbal), 5:04-10:37 (Got2Learn), 9:13-17:23 (Roger Wakefield), 12:45-18:14 (TheCheapFix), 16:22-22:36 (Arrow). Long formats are carried by live footage (Arrow) or many short items (TheCheapFix).
11. **Endings are short:** most outros are under 100 words.

### 4.1 Pointers for the plumbing rule pack, by dimension (for the pack writer to weigh)

| Dimension | What the competitor evidence points to |
|---|---|
| Viewer | Homeowners worried about cost and damage; tradespeople also watch and correct. |
| Skeleton | Title question or claim, answer inside the first minute, numbered items, one safety block, one honest limit, short close. Arrow's household-to-pro escalation is a second shape. |
| Steps | Quantities and wait times in every method. |
