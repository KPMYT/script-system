# Own channels: what separates the hits from the rest

Study date: 2026-10-20. Covers Roger the Pipe Guy, Mark Silverbridge Plumbing and The Vintage Pipefitter: 5 most-viewed videos from the last 24 months ("hits") and 5 most recent videos ("recent") per channel, 30 videos in all.

Sources: `comparison/stats.md`, `notes/INDEX.md`, the 30 notes files, the three combined transcripts (read in full), every `comments.md` and every `COLLECTION-LOG.md`.

How to read this:
- **Measured** means counted or timed from the data. **Inference** means my reading of it.
- Timestamps come from the transcripts and are accurate to within a few seconds. Quotes are verbatim auto-captions, so a few words may be misheard (for example "Sriracha" for Serratia).
- Comment counts are approximate. They come from a sample of about 40 comments per video (top plus newest), because the comment tool would not page further.

---

## 0. Limits: read this first

1. **Small samples.** Each group holds 5 videos. One odd video can change any median.
2. **Ratios are inflated on the small channels.** Views/normal divides by the channel's typical recent video: Roger 2,688, Mark 549, Vintage 609. Mark's "858×" hit has 471k views, fewer than Roger's 5th hit (451k at 168×). Compare absolute views across channels, not ratios.
3. **Recent videos are still growing.** At collection they were 1–8 days old (Vintage: 4 of 5 under 7 days). Within Mark's recent group the ratio roughly rises with age (0.47 at 1 day, 1.36 at 7 days). Recent-vs-recent comparisons mean little.
4. **Time and template are tangled.** All 15 hits were uploaded between 2026-02-14 and 2026-08-20. All 15 recent videos were uploaded between 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-19, and they all share a newer script template (details in section 4). Every gap between hits and recent could be the script, or the season, the algorithm, audience fatigue, or the thumbnail. These data cannot separate them.
5. **Not visible in this data:** thumbnails, click-through rate, audience retention, traffic sources, and the footage itself (only commenters describe it). Recent videos have 0–2 real comments each, so comments can only describe the hits.

## 3. Across the three channels

### 3.1 What all 15 hits share (measured unless marked)

1. **One cheap, concrete fix or a clear decision, with dollars attached.** 8 of 15 hits are about a household product (peroxide, washing soda, borax, citric acid). The rest are a clear decision or list (fittings, water heaters, traps, toilet install, drain don'ts). Every hit sets a repair cost against a fix cost.
2. **No name/channel intro at the start.** 0 of 15 hits; all 15 recent videos have one.
3. **A short mid-video ask.** All hits stayed at 76 words or fewer, and 7 asked only for a subscribe or like. All recent videos run 137–230 words, mostly a book pitch with a QR walkthrough.
4. **A tenure number is common** (9 of 15), and it contradicts something on all three channels (section 4).
5. **Long videos.** 13 of 15 hits run 15:43–23:09. Only Mark's sXlzedri_Hg (13:40) and Vintage's weakest hit 9_11vx_L6oM (14:25) are shorter. So long running time did not stop these videos. Length complaints still appear on all three channels.
6. **Viewers write their own summaries.** The top or a top-five comment is a recap or a skip-to time on WfAwg1l0xCE (1.5K likes), YjPAVZAffh0 (2K), hOz49worQJg (104), uWXIUmWdPeY (54) and Rb0aS97YnSA (49).
7. **The strong title word is walked back in the script** ("Every", "ANY", "Forever", "Dissolves") in kHT6ZL8At0w, rDLzNLUZnhw, hOz49worQJg and uWXIUmWdPeY. These were still hits, so this is about honesty and repeat trust, not reach.

### 3.2 Patterns specific to one channel

- **Mark:** head-to-head and decision topics (3 of 5 hits are "vs"), plain informational voice, the most AI and footage complaints.
- **Vintage:** Uncle Ray, "NOT X" twist titles, very long detail-packed hooks, script labels read aloud (section 4).
- **Roger:** habits and prevention, insider economics ("how he pays his mortgage"), the biggest audience. His recent videos also do far better in absolute terms (801–6.6k views against 121–1,016 on the others).
- **Comparison and verdict formats stand out in two places outside Mark's hits.** Vintage's best recent video is the ranked four-way (dIe837GgLDs, 1.67), and Roger's best recent is the "tried everything" attempts autopsy (8BZZwj93dkQ, 2.45). Inference: a verdict frame may travel across channels. With 5 videos per group this is a lead, not a finding.
