10. (10 likes) Soffits are a major flaw in construction. There are gaps at both ends of the soffit panels, especially if they are cut too short, where they tuck into the fascia cap and J channel. It allows wasps and all kinds of insects to enter. Another major flaw is between the edge of the roof sheathing and the top of the fascia board. The crack is never sealed up. If your house is under construction or you are replacing soffit, I recommend caulking and installing screening before the soffit is installed.
