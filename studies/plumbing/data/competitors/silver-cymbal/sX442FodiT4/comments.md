AI response. ✔ What to do when the generator runs out of gas When the generator stalls, your panel is suddenly de‑energized but all branch breakers are still ON. That means when you restart the generator, it will immediately be hit with the full house load — the worst possible startup condition. The safe reset procedure is: 1 Turn OFF the generator feed breaker Safety This isolates the panel from the generator before you restart it. Locate the generator backfeed breaker in the panel. Switch it fully OFF. Confirm the interlock still prevents the main breaker from being ON. 2 Turn OFF all branch breakers Recommended Prevents a massive inrush load when the generator comes back online. Turn OFF every breaker except the generator feed breaker. This ensures no appliances try to restart during generator startup. 3 Refuel and restart the generator Info Restarting under zero load protects the generator’s engine and alternator. Add fuel only when the generator is off and cooled. Restart the generator and let it stabilize for 30–60 seconds. 4 Turn ON the generator feed breaker Info This re‑energizes the panel from the generator source. Flip the generator backfeed breaker ON. Verify stable voltage from the generator. 5 Turn ON needed branch circuits one by one Recommended This avoids overload and lets you manage generator capacity. Start with essentials: fridge, boiler, lights. Add larger loads (A/C, well pump) last. Stop if the generator bogs down or voltage drops.
