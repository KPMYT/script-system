# Group D research: damage costs, licensing, DIY shelf

Researched 2026-10-21 (US). Method: WebSearch result snippets, plus WebFetch where the site allowed it (Fixr, This Old House, Bob Vila, Triple-I and the Texas board opened; HomeAdvisor, Angi and HomeGuide returned HTTP 403, so their figures come from search snippets of their pages).

**Read this first:**
1. **Searches ran out.** The shared web-search budget (200 calls per turn) ran out before the last items. These items were **not researched**: pipe repair clamp, SharkBite 1/2" push fitting, foam pipe insulation (6 ft), water leak detector, and most of the emergency kit. The outdoor faucet cover has only one retailer.
2. **Retail prices are snapshots.** They come from cached listings or search snippets, and most don't show when they were captured. "Year" for retail rows means "seen in an Oct 2026 search" unless a date is given. Third-party marketplace prices (often 2-3x the retailer's own price) are left out of ranges unless noted.
3. HomeGuide never came up in search results and blocks fetches. **No HomeGuide figures are used.**

### How to check a plumber's license

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Method | Get the license number (and in Texas the Responsible Master Plumber's number), then look it up on the state licensing board's website by name or number. Check that it's active, the expiration date, insurance on file and any discipline. The person must be listed on the license, not just the business. In some states plumbers are licensed by the city or county instead, so check the local building department. |
| Cost | free |
| Agreement | agree (CA, TX and FL boards give the same steps) |

Sources:
- Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners, "Consumer Information", https://tsbpe.texas.gov/consumer-information/ : verify "The validity of the Master Plumber license number" and "That the Master Plumber has a Certificate of Insurance on file"; Master numbers start with "M". Year not shown.
- Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners, "TSBPE HowTo PublicLicenseSearch" (PDF), https://tsbpe.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/TSBPE_HowTo_PublicLicenseSearch.pdf : the public search shows status, expiration, insurance, and disciplinary actions (snippet). 2023.
- California Contractors State License Board, "Contractors Board Warns Consumers About Unlicensed Contractors After Disasters", https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Media_Room/Disaster_Help_Center/Disaster_Information.aspx : the online License Check by name, business or license number shows "whether or not a license is active"; phone (800) 321-CSLB (2752); a license is needed for jobs of $1,000+; down payment limited to 10% or $1,000, whichever is less (snippet).
- Florida DBPR, "Unlicensed Activity: Dangers of Hiring an Unlicensed Person", https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/unlicensed-activity-dangers-of-hiring-an-unlicensed-person/ : "Users can verify licenses by name or license number" at https://www.myfloridalicense.com/portalsearches/VerifyLicensee ; phone (850) 487-1395.
