# Group A price research: service calls, drains, sewer, toilets

Researched 2026-10-21. National US figures, for rounded on-screen estimates only.

## How to read this

- **Fetched** means the page was opened directly and the figure read from it (Fixr, This Old House, Bob Vila, Inch Calculator, EPA, Denver Water).
- **Via search** means the figure came from the search engine's extract of the page, because the site blocked direct fetching (HomeAdvisor, Angi, HomeGuide, Forbes and HomeServe all returned HTTP 403). These figures are probably right, but they have not been read off the page itself. Check one before quoting it on camera.
- **Angi and HomeAdvisor are the same company** (Angi Inc.) and often show the same data. Where they are the only two sources, they count as **one publisher**. Bob Vila and This Old House often cite Angi too, and this is flagged where the page says so.
- HomeGuide was blocked and never showed up in search results, so no HomeGuide figures appear here.
- The session's shared web-search limit ran out before the last few items, so **bathroom sink stopper / pop-up drain** and **gurgling drain / vent clearing** were not researched. They are marked "not found", not guessed.
- Pro ranges below are rounded from the sources. "Typical" means where most sources overlap, not the absolute low or high.
