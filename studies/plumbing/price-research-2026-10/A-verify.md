# Group A price check (second, independent checker)

Checked 2026-10-21 against `A-research.md`. National US figures, for rounded on-screen estimates only.

## How this check was done

- I only counted publishers the researcher did **not** use for that job. The main ones were **Thumbtack**, **Modernize**, **HomeGuide**, **Forbes Home**, **Today's Homeowner** and **Home Depot** (for parts). I also used **EPA** pages the researcher had not cited, plus UF/IFAS Extension.
- **Read live** means I downloaded the page and read the figure off it myself: Thumbtack, Modernize, Today's Homeowner, Home Depot, EPA, UF/IFAS and Bob Vila. **Via search** means the figure came from the search engine's summary, because the site blocks downloads (HomeGuide, Forbes and Angi/HomeAdvisor all returned 403). Treat via-search figures as probably right but not read off the page.
- **A caveat on Thumbtack.** Thumbtack's "price by clog location" table (kitchen sink $100-$225, toilet $110-$275, main line $100-$800) is almost the same as HomeAdvisor's table. Its hydro-jetting line ($350-$600, up to $1,000) is also word-for-word Angi. Those lines may be copied, so I did not count them as independent. Thumbtack's headline "according to Thumbtack data" ranges are its own figures, and I did count those.
- I used about 33 web searches in all, well under the budget.

## Out-of-date or easy-to-dispute figures

- **Sewer replacement "average $3,320"** (This Old House/Angi). A viewer who has priced a full line will dispute it. Modernize's average is $5,000, HomeServe's is $6,633, and Forbes puts trenched jobs at $4,000-$20,000. Use the range, not this average.
- **Hydro jetting "$350-$600"** comes from one data source: Angi, repeated by Bob Vila, Fixr's second page and Thumbtack. Forbes and HomeGuide run higher. Give the higher top end.
- **Wax ring "$60" and flapper "$50" low ends.** A plumber pulling and resetting a toilet for $60 is not believable once the service call is included. Independent sources start at about $100 for a wax ring and $75 for a flapper.
- **The rocking-toilet "$50-$150"** has no independent source and sits below most service-call minimums.
- **Pre-2024 sources still cited:** Inch Calculator (Sep 2023); Bob Vila sewer replacement (Jan 2023); the EPA family fact sheet PDF (2017), which should be swapped for the current EPA climate page (Jan 2026) with the same 200-gallon line; Denver Water (Mar 2023). Angi's emergency page is "2024 Data", and Forbes's hydro-jet and plumbing-repair pages are about 2024. The figures still agree with current sources, but the citations are old.
- **The running-toilet "$400" top end** is wider than the independent sources ($100-$300), so drop it.
- **Service call "$100-$250"**: Modernize and HomeServe put the plain call-out fee nearer $50-$100. If the script says "$100 to $250 just to show up", some viewers will say theirs was cheaper. "Often $100 or more" is safer.
