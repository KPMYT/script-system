# Group E price research: the gaps from groups A to D

Researched 2026-10-21. National US figures, for rounded on-screen estimates only ("about $150", "anywhere from $200 to $400 depending on where you live").

## How to read this

- **Read on page** means the page was opened directly and the figure read from it. This worked for Fixr, This Old House (TOH), Bob Vila, Consumer Reports, PickHVAC, gocodebook (California Plumbing Code) and the Jurupa Community Services District.
- **Via search summary** means the figure came from the search engine's extract of the page, because the site blocked direct reads. HomeGuide, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Forbes, Home Depot, Lowe's, SupplyHouse, HomeServe and ecode360 all returned HTTP 403 this round. Home Depot product and search pages were blocked too, though group B could read them. These figures are probably right but were not read off the page. Check one on the live page before a script names the source.
- **Angi and HomeAdvisor are one company** and count as one source. Bob Vila's cost guides cite Angi, and TOH sometimes repeats Angi or matches Fixr word for word. Each case is flagged below.
- **Old pages:** Fixr's burst-pipe page (updated Aug 2022) and Bob Vila's repipe page (Apr 2023) are older than the 2024-2026 window. Their figures appear only to back up newer ones, and each is marked.
- Store prices are search snapshots from different dates. Treat them as October 2026, give or take.
- About 66 searches were used out of a 200 budget. Where a cost guide simply does not price a job, the job is marked "not found", and no figure was guessed.

### 8a. Healthy household water pressure and code maximum (non-price facts)

| Fact | Figure |
|---|---|
| Healthy range | 40-60 psi, about 50 psi ideal (many sources call 40-80 psi the outer "normal" range) |
| Code maximum | 80 psi static. Above that, a pressure-reducing valve is required (IPC 604.8; UPC/California Plumbing Code 608.2) |

Sources:
- This Old House, "How to Install a Water Pressure Reducing Valve", https://www.thisoldhouse.com/plumbing/21017124/how-to-install-a-water-pressure-reducing-valve: ideal "40 to 60 PSI"; about 50 PSI; above 80 PSI is high. Updated May 25, 2026. Read on page.
- Jurupa Community Services District (California water utility), "Water Pressure", https://www.jcsd.us/yourdistrict/water/aboutjcsdwater/water-pressure/: "Setting your water pressure at 60psi or lower will help conserve water and extend the life of plumbing fixtures"; most homeowners set about 50 psi; above 80 psi you are "required to have and maintain a water pressure regulator device on your property"; "Regulators are the responsibility of the property owner." Undated. Read on page.
- ICC, "2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) - 604.8 Water pressure-reducing valve or regulator.", https://codes.iccsafe.org/s/IPC2021P1/chapter-6-water-supply-and-distribution/IPC2021P1-Ch06-Sec604.8: "Where water pressure within a building exceeds 80 psi (552 kPa) static, an approved water pressure-reducing valve conforming to ASSE 1003 or CSA B356 with strainer shall be installed to reduce the pressure in the building water distribution piping to 80 psi (552 kPa) static or less." Exception for lines to sillcocks and outside hydrants. Via search summary.
- gocodebook, 2025 California Plumbing Code (based on the UPC) § 608.2 Excessive Water Pressure, https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/ca-plumbing-code/chapter-6-water-supply-and-distribution/608.2-excessive-water-pressure.-where-static-water: "Where static water pressure in the water supply piping exceeds 80 psi (552 kPa)... an approved-type pressure regulator preceded by an adequate strainer shall be installed". Also requires an expansion tank downstream. Effective Jan 1, 2026. Read on page.
- Manufacturer settings (supporting): Zurn Wilkins 34-NR3XL, factory preset 50 psi, adjustable 15-75 psi (https://pexuniverse.com/wilkins-34-nr3xl); Watts LF25AUB-Z3, 50 psi set point, adjustable 25-75 psi. Via search summary.

**Agreement:** agree. Script-safe: "most homes do best around 50 psi. Plumbing code says anything over 80 needs a pressure regulator."
