## Method notes

- **Dates:** NexLev gives relative dates such as "3 months ago". These were turned into month-precise dates set to the 28th.
  - "1 year ago" becomes 2025-10-28, but it can mean anywhere from 12 to 23 months ago.
  - A channel whose newest upload shows only as "1 year ago" is not counted as active in the last 12 months. That applied to Wise Women Over 60, Hairstyleslife and Mathias4Makeup.
- **on_topic for titles with no age angle:** hair or fashion videos on 50+ or partly-50+ channels count as `core`, noted "no age in title". The same applies to the broad hair channels (Hair Cut, Blowout Professor). Makeup with no age angle counts as `adjacent`, with the same note.
- **Plus-size fashion** with no 50+ angle on a broad channel counts as `off` (SuppleChicTV).
- **Type `unclear`** is used for:
  - salon or client footage with no presenter (Her Hair, Hair Cut)
  - the same photographic presenter shown but never named (Living Your Best Life; Blowout Professor, a man)
- **Mathias4Makeup:** the presenter is a man, named only through the channel name. It is typed `real_on_camera` with that note.
- **Thumbnails:** where the outlier tool returned no outliers (Tarantula, Mathias4Makeup), the two thumbnails were taken from the newest list.

## Deviations

- **A 4th call** (`youtube_channel_videos`, sort popular) was made on borderline channels. Borderline means all four: under 10K subscribers, no 100K+ video in the scan, older than a year, and more than 100 videos. The extra call was made on Alice Luxe, Fashion ideas, Ageless 360, Susan's Sweet Life and Living Your Best Life.
  - Popular-sort videos at 2x or more of the NexLev average that were not already in the scan were added as outlier rows. They have a blank `nexlev_ratio` and the topic note "popular sort".
  - This added 33 rows with a blank NexLev ratio.
  - It changed the keep result for Alice Luxe (490K pixie video), Susan's Sweet Life (143K) and Living Your Best Life (319K).
- **Blank normal:** Biofest and Hairstyleslife have no long-form uploads in their newest 30, so `normal` is blank. Their outlier rows have a blank `ratio_vs_normal`.
- **Fewer than 15 videos in the normal:** where fewer than 15 long-form videos qualified, the normal uses all that did, and `normal_basis` says so (for example Mathias4Makeup, with 5).
- **Retyped:** Fashion fiesta and Biofest were changed to `faceless_narrated` after their thumbnails were read. CRLF line endings were restored in the CSVs after an early fix.
- **Title fix:** one Ageless Refinement title had its `|` swapped by a helper-script delimiter. It was restored to the original text.

  - church and Sunday outfits
  - "Things That Scream Old Lady"
- **Melanin cluster.** Timeless Melanin, Melanin Fashion Secrets and Melanin & Style After 60 share titles.
- **Dating problems.**
  - On some channels NexLev dates every video "1 year ago".
  - Ageless 360 is a 2010 account whose uploads all date from around 2025.
  - BIG WOW shows a joined date of 2026-09.
  - Fashion Mantika has a title that says "2027".
- **Titles that don't mean what they seem.** BIG WOW uses "50+" as a count of outfits, not an age. Clara Kensington's titles mention looks made with AI.
- **Mismatched channels.** Last Laugh Recaps was renamed and its about text doesn't match its content. KlarerBlick has German-language thumbnails.
- **NexLev average far from recent views.** Fashion fiesta's NexLev average is 102K, while its recent uploads get 2–11K. Blowout Professor's newest 30 put 17 videos at "1 year ago" despite a monthly upload rhythm.
- **The outlier scan misses older hits.** It covers only the 100 most recent videos, so hits such as Mathias4Makeup's operator-list video ("Makeup on Women over 60", 5.3M, about 11 years ago) don't appear.
- **Personal emails.** These appear in several about texts and were not stored; the notes say "email flag (not stored)".
