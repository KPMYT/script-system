## Odd things seen (descriptions, not judgements)

- NexLev's avgViews is a lifetime average, and all dates are coarse (derived from text like '2 months ago'), so many uploads share the same date.
- NexLev's faceless check disagrees with the thumbnails on several channels: it says 'not faceless' for channels whose thumbnails look generated (Chic Fashion Over 50, Stylish Chic women, Grace After 60, Timeless Glow Club, Timeless Elegant Fashion), and 'faceless' for Shepherd's Fashions, whose staff are on camera.
- Several old channels appear repurposed for new 50+ content (The Grace Edit 2020, Graceful Over 60 2010, Chic Fashion Over 50 2024).
- Re-uploads of the same title with the same length: Ageless Elegance (several haircut videos), Timeless Glow Club (two titles).
- Ebony lifestyle's top thumbnail mirrors Golden Melanin Lifestyle's.
- Megan Graham: 25 of the newest 30 uploads show no view count.
- Care with Kate: lifetime average of about 370K views per video against a best long-form video of 50K in the newest 30 (most views probably from Shorts, reading).
- Normals resting on few qualifying uploads: Trendy Outfit Vibes (2), Glam Guide (3), Megan Graham (4); their ratios are inflated accordingly.
- Earlier channels (see rows 1-33): Salud de Hierro has filename-style titles ('CECILIA 14 FINAL'); Rosalind Gray's handle does not match its name and its about is a code; ICONIC uploads only ~3-minute clips with a fair-use notice; Camino de los Ancianos and Salud de Hierro mix Spanish thumbnails or about text with English titles.
