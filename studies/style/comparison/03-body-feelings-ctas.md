## What this can't show

- **Pictures.** No footage was viewed. Everything about visuals is what the narration says; whether the pictures matched (the lawn-product logos on Vivian Sinclair, the slim young models viewers complain about) is known only from comments.
- **Cause.** Nothing here shows that any feeling, slogan, softener, CTA or price line made a video win. The winners differ from ours in presenter, channel age, subscribers, topic, title, thumbnail and length at once; pair comparisons are in `05-copycat-pairs.md`.
- **Our numbers are tiny.** 57 comments on 38 videos (15 our own links), most videos under a month old, five Scarlet uploads under 7 days. *No comment ask* on Scarlet is mostly her newest uploads, so it can't be read against views.
- **Counts are approximate.** Pattern searches miss paraphrase and catch words used about objects ("The chain disappears into the net", w6w_ktMBHXM [7:13]); the "read" counts are one reader's call on which lines are about the woman. Auto-captions mishear (*Bato*; "the old woman goes the color of a rainy Tuesday", jPoeJlLO2_w [11:13], may be *whole*).
- **Descriptions are partial.** Only what the collectors recorded in `meta.md` (first lines and notes) was available; a promise *in the description* was checked against those records, not the live page.
- **Who is AI.** The AI-presenter group rests on round 2 thumbnail readings and comments; Ageless Confidence was never typed. No study video discloses AI, so "real" versus "AI" is our reading, not the channels' statement.
- **One to four transcripts per channel.** A presenter's habit (Marie-Anne's *watch the video on screen*, Diana GOSS's *see you on Sunday*) is seen in 2-4 videos, not her catalogue.
- **The 15 winners** are chosen by raw views, which favours old, large real channels; by views ÷ normal (where a normal exists) the list would lean faceless.
