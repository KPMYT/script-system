# The copycat pairs' thumbnails (main session, 2026-10-28)

The main session looked at the eight pair videos' current thumbnails (`hqdefault.jpg`, read with the image viewer, not stored). This adds to `05-copycat-pairs.md`, which had no thumbnail reading. It is a description, not a verdict.

| Pair | Winner's thumbnail | Copy's thumbnail |
|---|---|---|
| **A**: Age with Style, 1.1M against 7K, same channel | Clothes rail and a blurred woman; text in yellow and white: "Stop Wearing Your Blouses Like This After 60+ They Age You!" | **The same image and text, pixel for pixel.** |
| **B**: ChicAfterSixty 493K against Ageless Confidence 1K | One generated woman of about 60, smiling and pointing at the text; "STOP STYLING FINE HAIR AFTER 60 DO THIS INSTEAD" | A before and after: the same woman with flat gray hair, then smiling with full silver hair. Nearly the same text. |
| **C**: Chic After 60 960K against Elegant After 60 2K | A city street with no person; text only, "Older Women Who Look Young And Elegant Allways Wear These 8 Color Combos" (with the misspelling "Allways") | A generated woman in a camel coat with a red arrow and a red box, "8 Color Combos"; text "60+ Women Who Look Young & Elegant Always Wear These" |
| **D**: Marie-Anne Lecoeur 1.0M (real) against Ageless Style 50s & 60s 267K | Two photos of the presenter (one pointing, one in a busy floral blouse) on hot pink; three words, "OLD LADY LOOKS" | A blurred woman in a kitchen; the full title as text, "7 Things That Scream 'Old Lady' (Harsh But True)" |

**What this shows, and what it doesn't:**
- **Pair A rules out the title, the thumbnail and the channel.** The two uploads share all three. That leaves the script, the upload date (the copy came about a month later) and how YouTube treats a repeated upload. The study can't separate those.
- **In B and C, the thumbnail doesn't line up with the result.** The copy's thumbnail carries more of the devices the niche uses (a before and after, a person with an arrow and a red box) than the winner's. In C, the winner's thumbnail is plain text with a misspelling.
- **The thumbnail text is not the title** in A, C and D. It is a shorter, harder line: "They Age You!", "OLD LADY LOOKS".
- Four pairs are too few to say what decides which copy breaks out. The channel's own normal (higher for the winner in all three cross-channel pairs) is the one consistent factor outside the script.
