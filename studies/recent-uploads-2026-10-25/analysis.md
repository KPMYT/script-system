# Analysis: the seven most recent uploads (2026-10-25)

The main session's reading of the transcripts in `transcripts/` (the captions of what the voice said), against the woodworking study (`HANDOFF.md` §4), the plumbing study (`studies/plumbing/comparison/`) and the library's own rules. Findings only: no rule has changed because of this file. The proposed changes are in `drafts/tts-hook-and-length-DRAFT.md`.

**Other sounds in the audio:** the captions mark "[clears throat]", "[snorts]", "[gasps]" and "[sighs and gasps]" in Roger (both videos), Mark, Dale and Sam. These were already there before the library: Roger's ten older study videos carry 5 "[snorts]" and 4 "[clears throat]", Mark's 4 and Vintage's 5. They come from the voices, not from the new scripts.

## 4. The lower average view duration

What these seven can and can't say:

- **The videos got shorter, so average view duration in minutes falls on its own.** Roger's two newest average 13.9 minutes against 24.2 for his previous 23 uploads; Vintage's recent ones 15.1 against 20.1; Mark's 17.7 against 19.7; Sam's 15.4 against 19.0 (`README.md`). If viewers watch the same share of the video, a 14-minute video earns about 40% less view duration than a 24-minute one. **The number to compare is average percentage viewed**, at the same age (first 7 days) against the channel's own normal (`PINNED.md`), with click-through checked separately.
- **The 12-minute plumbing minimum isn't 12 minutes.** The plumbing profiles leave words per minute unset, so the script-writer counts at 150 (1,800 words). The voices now read at about 167 to 190 words a minute (Roger 167-170, Mark 185, Vintage 190 on these videos; their older study videos ran about 152, 159 and 164). So a script that "passes" at 1,800 words plays in about 9½ to 11 minutes, which is how Vintage's 2,141 words came out at 11:15. Hank (185), Dale (170) and Sam (174) have theirs set.
- **Likely script-side causes, in the order I'd fix them:**
  1. Lumber sizes misread by the voice (Sam), in the opening.
  2. The plumbing openings run 55 to 75 seconds before item one, because of the promise block.
  3. Long stretches before the title's payoff: Mark's fixes at 8:17; Hank's 2½ minutes of regulations mid-build.
  4. Thin back halves when a title's count outruns the content (Roger's 12 reasons).
- **Dale's video probably didn't come from the library** (section 3), so it can't be counted for or against it.
