# Voice samples: woodworking for profit

The voice anchor for every script in this lane. These are three stretches of real human narration from winning videos, copied verbatim from transcripts. Only obvious transcription errors are fixed, and stage noises are removed. Test every sentence of a draft against these (core `voice-anchoring.md`).

**Borrow:** how the sentences are built. That means the rhythm, the second-person instructions, the way prices and measurements are dropped in mid-sentence, and the short asides.

**Don't borrow:** the content, or any first-person claim ("I've been earning with tools…," "like I typically do," "cost me"). Those are these creators' lives. In our scripts, first-person build narration becomes an instruction to the viewer (the skill's step rules), and personal claims follow the claims policy.
