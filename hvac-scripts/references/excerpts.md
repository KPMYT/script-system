# Excerpt library: how the winners say each kind of thing (HVAC)

Short lines from winning HVAC and home-comfort scripts, grouped by the job the line does. Each one shows its source as (channel, video ID, [timestamp], the video's views against its channel normal, its tag).

**Sources:** the 36 transcripts in `studies/hvac/data/` (WINNI's cold-walls video has no transcript): 25 videos from 14 real-person channels, 10 faceless or persona videos (Ray Dutton's Garage ×6, HVAC Exposed ×2, Vent Exposed, Lost Survival Knowledge) and Roger the Pipe Guy's own AC drain video. Views against normal come from `studies/hvac/notes/INDEX.md` (collected 2026-10-27). Normals differ hugely between channels (about 550 views on HVAC Exposed, about 1.9 million on Technology Connections), so compare a ratio only with others on the same channel. grayfurnaceman's ratios are against an off-topic baseline.

**How quotes are given:** verbatim auto-captions, checked by script against the transcript. The timestamp is the start of the caption line where the quote begins. "..." marks a cut, and [square brackets] mark an obvious caption fix. Sixteen transcripts have no punctuation, so their lines read as one long run.

**Two tags:**
- **[real]:** a real person's channel (grayfurnaceman is faceless, but a human voice and a 35-year serviceman). Borrow the voice and the structure: how plain the line is, how short, and where it sits.
- **[structure only]:** a faceless, AI-voiced or persona script (Ray Dutton's Garage, HVAC Exposed, Vent Exposed, Lost Survival Knowledge, Roger the Pipe Guy). Borrow how the line is built and where it sits, never its voice or its wording.

**How to use this file:** to learn the range of shapes each job can take, never to copy wording. Every group holds several shapes. Pick a different one from the last script on the same channel (the rotation log tracks it) and write it fresh from the brief, in the persona's voice. Every fact, price, reading and wait in a script comes from the brief and the price guide, never from a line quoted here. The scanner's copy guard flags any run of 8 or more words shared with this file.

**What to leave behind on the real channels:** they have a face, their own hands, a meter and a furnace on camera. Anything that needs the footage ("right here", "like this one", "as you can see", a meter reading), their names, greetings ("Hey guys, Jay here"), sign-offs ("you guys be safe"), stores, sponsors, codes and their own bench tests stays with them. Roger never claims a test.

**What to leave behind on the faceless channels:** Ray Dutton's sound is easy to spot and some viewers already hear it as AI. His catchphrases never appear in a Roger script: "keep your cool", "25 years ... for the same companies", "quietly" (once a script at most), "one plain guide ... no pressure", "I'll be straight with you", "None of this is hidden because...", "if this saved you a call", "I'm going to argue against myself". HVAC Exposed's named customers ("a homeowner I'll call David") are customer stories the pack bans.

**Problem lines:** a few groups end with one or two lines marked **Problem:**. They show what our rules ban (a motive, a tease, pointing at the screen, a test we can't claim, unsafe advice, Ray's catchphrases) or what the comments punished. Never use them as a model.

## 8. The mechanism in one plain line

Notice that the best ones are under 20 words, use kitchen words (a battery, a hairdryer, a glass of iced tea), and either stand alone or answer a "why" asked the line before.
