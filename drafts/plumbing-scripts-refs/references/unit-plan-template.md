# Unit plan template: plumbing

Fill this in from the title and brief before writing any prose. It decides what the script contains, in what order, and roughly how long it runs. It holds content, never sentences.

```
UNIT PLAN
Title:
Channel:
Video type:                        [one of the ten in SKILL.md, or "new type; closest is ..."]
Years figure:                      [none / the title's number / the profile's number]

OPENING                            [order this time, from the video type: ...]
- the point, first sentence:       [the subject, and the fix or product named]
- question (optional):             [one the viewer answers in their own house; answered within ~15 s]
- villain line:                    [the trade's price or silence, from the brief]
- scope or reassurance:            [what it won't do, or why it's safe]
- promise block:                   [2-3 later items, each with its stake -> where each is paid]
- first real tip by:               [seconds; in a list, item one by ~30 s]
- tenure:                          [not used / where, and the job it does there]

ITEMS                              [one line each]
- [item] -> shape: ... | stake: ... | money line? | safety line? | forward line into the next item
- priority flag ("if you do only one thing") on:

MONEY
- the comparison within 2 min:     [fix range vs the trade's range, both from the brief]
- device this time:                [three prices side by side / trade price per item / running total / payback]
- cost per use, with the dose arithmetic:
- what was avoided, for a money figure in the title:

TESTS AND RESULTS                  [only those the brief gives; said without "I"]

SAFETY
- lines the brief gives:
- defaults used where the brief is silent:
- call-a-pro trigger:              [a named sign, never "if it's outside your comfort zone"]

CTAs AND ENDING
- mid CTA after:                   [the first real payoff] (Roger, Mark; none on Vintage)
- end reminder:                    one line
- comment line (the last line):    [a specific question from the viewer's house, + "I read every one"; not last video's]

PROMISES CHECK
- each promise -> where it's paid  [the first within ~5 min; none held past the middle except the safety rule or a second use]
- longest stretch without a forward line or a payoff:

LENGTH ESTIMATE
- items by type, rough words -> minutes at this channel's wpm   [12 minimum; 12-20 the sweet spot]
- can't reach 12 minutes with real value? -> NOT READY: ask for a fuller brief; never pad

VARIATION CHECK                    [against the rotation log]
- opening order and shape:
- promise devices:
- money device:
- stock phrases planned (each once):
- comment question:
```
