Six complete scripts of the biggest AI-voiced plumbing hits in the study: five from our own channels and one from TheCheapFix, a faceless AI competitor. Each is verbatim (auto-captions, with their mistakes kept) and labelled with this pack's plumbing skeleton, so you can see end to end how a winning opening is ordered, how the promise block is built, how items are shaped and paced, and where money and CTAs sit.

> **STRUCTURE ONLY.** Read this file at the planning step, while filling in the unit plan, for the *shape* of a winner: the order of the opening beats, how the promise block names its items and stakes, how long each item runs and what goes inside it, where the money lines and the CTA land. **Never borrow voice or wording.** These scripts were AI-written, so their sentences are not a model for ours; the voice comes from the persona profile and `voice-samples.md`. The real (human) channels' scripts are kept separately (`archive/`); use those, with `voice-samples.md`, for how a sentence should sound.

That is seven distinct payoffs promised before item one, the most of any own hit. Its weak spot is length: at 315 words it is the wordiest opening of the six, and item one waits until 1:46. The items are even units of 260-320 words (about 95-125 seconds), each in the same order: a scene from the viewer's house, the method with amounts and waits, one mechanism line, the stake.
