# H01: the hook reference, Paris Never Ages (a competitor)

A competitor's AI persona channel (27.8K subscribers, seven videos from 2026-10-31 to 11-05, 27K to 782K views each; NexLev, 2026-11-06). The operator picked it on 2026-10-31 for how its hooks trigger the feeling ("paris does them so much better", "I LOVE how paris does this") and again on 2026-11-06. **Read for the hook's beats only.** Never its lines, its narrator, her backstory or her stories.

- **Source:** `studies/style/competitors/paris-never-ages/` (captions via NexLev, 2026-11-06)
- **Take from it: the beats, in order.** Every one of its hooks runs the same way, in about 150 words before its persona intro and item one:
  1. the title made personal and present: the problem is in her closet, or on her face, right now, and the trigger is said outright (adds years, takes the light out of the face)
  2. the woman who gets it right wouldn't wear it
  3. not because of what the viewer can't change (not richer, not thinner): she simply knows what each one does
  4. within reach: a swap this week, often from her own closet
  5. the result in her own mirror, at once
  6. the blame off her in one strong line (it's never the woman, it's the cut, the color, the fabric), sometimes with the moment she lived (caught in the mirror, asking why she looks tired)
  7. together, into item one ("let's start with...")
- **Don't take:** the persona intro and her name, "thirty years with the clothes of a Paris couture house", the dated stories with names and streets (Diane may run that device only with stories from the brief), the optician disclaimer, anything that copies its rhythm word for word.
