# Hook bank: style

Every model's hook (from the first word to the first word of item one), broken into its moves, with its length, the seconds to item one and the feeling it aims at. Built 2026-10-29 from the 37 files in `models/`. The operator's instruction: "the hooks should be very much format carried from our reference and just applied. not long, clear to the point and triggering the right emotions".

## How to use this file

1. Pick one hook of the same video type as the title, or the closest type, from an M entry (Yes, or In part with only the moves it names) where one fits, and never the hook model of any of the channel's last five scripts.
2. Carry its moves in its order and apply them to this title with fresh words. Add no move; drop one only when the title has nothing for it.
3. Keep it no longer than the model's hook as kept, and never over about 110 words. Where a model runs longer, carry its moves inside 110 words.
4. Aim it at the title's feeling, named early in her words. Hair: the fear of the change, the cut that never looks right, the stylist who didn't listen. Fashion: older or frumpier than she feels, the body, clothes not made for her. Then the relief: permission, or the why.
5. F hooks ([structure only]) lend their moves only. Their wording is not shown.
6. Never reuse wording: the copy guard flags any 8 words in a row shared with this file.

**How the entries are built.** The hook runs from the start of the model's script to the first word of item one. Passages that are only a banned move (an intro, a tease, a sponsor read, a subscribe ask, a CTA, a recap, an age slogan, a production note, or a passage carrying only banned labels such as a try-on or a client) are replaced by "[banned move omitted: <label>]" and listed under the quote. A passage that does a real move but also carries a banned element (M01's stylist blame) is kept and flagged: carry the move, never the flagged part. Words are the spoken words kept. Seconds are the model's timestamp for item one, with the time left once the omitted parts are taken out. Moves use one short vocabulary: feared look named; the feeling named; the cause; reassurance or scope; question to her; the promise word; count line; credential; permission; contrast (wrong vs right); a quick example; item one (plus a few named where a model does something else). **Usable:** Yes (the whole format fits a persona), In part (only the moves named), No (it depends on something a persona can't do), Moves only (F files).
