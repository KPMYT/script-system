# Model library: style

37 winning videos, sorted by the eight video types in `SKILL.md` ("The video types"), then "other". 30 are real presenters (M01 to M30) and 7 are faceless or AI videos (F01 to F06, F08). Each file holds a beat map (what each stretch of the video does, with timings, items, the title's promise, feelings, villain, hard words and CTA placement; no wording), how the video voices each emotion, why it works (with the top comments), the full labelled script, lines worth studying ([real] files) and a "Doesn't transfer" list. Every model's hook, broken into its moves, is in `../hooks.md`.

- **What they are:** picks, views, dates and channel normals are in `studies/style/models/approved-models.csv`; views were counted on 2026-10-28. The operator's rules (HANDOFF section 3, 2026-10-28 and session 13, P18): real people presenting to women over 50, outliers of the last 24 months plus a few big older ones, nothing before 2022 (the oldest is M25, posted 2022-10-04), and a faceless section on topics the real models don't cover. F07 was dropped (mostly music).
- **[real] (M files):** borrow the structure, the voice and the emotion. **Never the wording.** The copy guard flags any run of 8 or more words shared with a model file.
- **[structure only] (F files):** borrow the shape only (order, item count and length, pacing, devices). Never the voice, the wording or the narrator's invented persona (the claimed years, family, clients and past of F01, F02, F05 and F06).
- **Our presenters are different:** Scarlett Starling and Diane de Chambray are AI talking-head presenters with no age, no career story, no clients and no closet of their own. Every try-on, client, salon story, sponsor read, price on a non-budget title, greeting, tease and subscribe ask in these videos stays with its creator. Each file's labels mark them ([TRY-ON: banned], [CLIENT: banned], [INTRO: banned], [TEASE: banned], [SALON BLAME: banned], [SPONSOR] and the rest).

**How to use them** (SKILL.md, "Using the models: never a copy"). A script borrows their structure, never their wording, and never one model's structure whole.
1. **Read "Doesn't transfer" first, then the labels.** Treat every passage with a banned label as if it weren't there.
2. **Name the models and the differences:** one or two models of the title's video type (or an "also fits" model), and at least three things done differently: the opening order, the number or order of items, the item shape, the turn, the comment question.
3. **The hook comes from `../hooks.md`:** one model's hook format, carried move by move, no longer than the model's and never over about 110 words.
4. **No repeats:** never a model from the channel's last five rotation log lines (`| model: [IDs]`), and never the same hook model (`| hook: [ID]`).
5. **Blend where it helps:** one model's opening, another's item pacing.
6. **Planning only:** close the file before writing. The wording comes fresh from the brief, in the persona's voice.

**Read with care:**
- **M05 (RaDona):** a salon cut-along with a client. Voice and emotion only, for the client over 70 (the reassurance lines); never the structure.
- **The Justin Hickox files (M06, M08, M10, M12):** a man presenting. Structure and emotion only; never a voice sample. M08 and M12 are sponsored by a dryer maker and styled on a client or model.
- **F files (F01 to F06, F08):** structure only. F01 and F02 (Vivian Sinclair), F05 (French Fashion) and F06 (Emiko Sato) speak through an invented persona with years, clients or family: labelled [INVENTED PERSONA: banned].
- **F06:** the national lens model ("N rules Japanese women follow") for Diane de Chambray's "French way" titles. Its topic, hygiene, is outside the pack: borrow the shape, never the topic or its claims.
- **The thin types:** never these, wear these instead has no real-presenter accessory list (glasses and earrings are F01 and F02, structure only); how to wear X has one model (M30); the rules she never breaks has only F06 filed there (M28 and F05 also fit). Where a type runs out under the no-repeat rule, use the closest type's model and say so in the plan.
- **Try-ons:** many real presenters wear the looks themselves (heaviest in M14, M20, M02, M17, M25, M11, M15, M24, M26, M27, M29, M30). Borrow the order and the why; the proof is a try-on and stays with them.
- **Sponsors and plugs:** sponsor reads in M08, M12, M14, M15, M24 and M28; a one-brand haul with affiliate links in M19; free guides, quizzes or paid services in M01, M13, M24 and M27. The book is our only CTA.
- **Not aimed at women over 50:** M28 (a general shopping audience), M14 (over 40), and M01, M07 and M09 (no age said). Their shapes transfer; the over-50 framing comes from the brief.
- **Views against normal** compare a video with its own channel only.
