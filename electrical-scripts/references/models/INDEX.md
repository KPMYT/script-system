# Model library: electrical

31 winning videos from real, on-camera channels, sorted by the seven video types in `SKILL.md` ("The video types"). Each file holds a beat map (what each stretch of the video does, with timings, items, promise devices, stakes, safety lines and CTA placement; no wording), how the video voices each emotion, why it works (with the top comments), the full labelled script and a "Doesn't transfer" list.

- **What they are:** every video has 1M+ views; 27 of the 31 were posted in 2022 or later (E08, E12, E17 and E23 are 2020-2021). Picks, views and dates come from NexLev on 2026-10-27 (`studies/electrical/model-picks-2026-10-27.csv`). E06 was dropped: a working electrician's panel job, not homeowner work.
- **All are [real]:** made by real people filming their own hands. Borrow the structure and the voice: where each beat sits, how long it runs, how plain the line is. **Never the wording.** The copy guard flags any run of 8 or more words shared with a model file.
- **Our narrator is different:** Frederick Barnes is an AI talking-head presenter who explains and never demonstrates. Every demonstration, meter reading, job story, price, sponsor read and bit of pointing in these videos stays with its creator. Each file's labels mark them ([POINTING: banned], [FIRST-PERSON DEMO: banned], [MONEY: banned for this channel] and the rest).

**How to use them** (SKILL.md, "Using the models: never a copy"):
1. At the planning step, pick one or two models for the video type that aren't in the channel's last five rotation log lines (`| model: [IDs]`).
2. Read the file's "Doesn't transfer" section first, then the beat map.
3. Name at least three things this script does differently: the opening order, the number or order of items, the promise devices, the stake device, the comment question.
4. Blend where it helps: one model's opening shape, another's step pacing.
5. Close the file before writing. The wording comes fresh from the brief, in the persona's voice.

**Read with care:**
- **UK and European channels:** E07, E10 and E27 are UK (sockets, earth, consumer unit, Part P, millimetre sizes, brown and blue colors); E15 is filmed in France (230 volts, 50 hertz, RCD, a ground-rod earthing system). Their wording, voltages and code never transfer; the US terms come from the brief and the facts guide.
- **E21** has a German presenter: metric sizes, kilograms and European lever-connector lines. Only the wire-nut side is US.
- **E20** is a science hobbyist in his lab, not an electrician or a homeowner channel. The question transfers; the lab doesn't.
- **Trade bashing:** labelled [TRADE BASHING: banned] in E18 (the title and 1:01-1:31), E20, E24, E25 and E32; flagged in E16's "Doesn't transfer" (the viewer's electrician friend set up as the one who got it wrong). Our narrator never runs down electricians, inspectors, firefighters or the utility.
- **Unsafe on-camera methods:** labelled [UNSAFE: banned] in E03 (live probing, a meter in series on a live cord), E12 (meter probes on a breaker's terminal), E19 (shorting an outlet, live probing), E20 (touching a Tesla coil's arc), E25 (the panel cover off with the panel live), E28 (the knot splice called permanent) and E30 (the breaker turned back on to find a wire with the box open). Never narrate any of them.
- **No power-off or prove-it-dead step:** E05, E09 (one six-word clause, no breaker and no tester), E23, E24, E26 and E29 do wiring work with no power-off line at all. E04, E12, E21, E28 and E32 have none either (tool, bench and panel videos). Ours always says it, inside the first step that needs it (SKILL.md, "Safety").
- **Money:** prices are labelled in E02, E03, E04, E05, E08, E18, E21, E22, E25, E26, E28, E29 and E32. This channel says no prices at all.
- **Sponsor reads** (omitted or labelled): E05, E12, E13, E14, E19, E21.
- **Slips in the source:** some files list factual slips to check against the brief (E16 at 5:02, E14's garbled caption numbers, E19's mis-heard part names). Read the end of each file.
- **Views against normal** compare a video with its own channel only. Normals range from 3,950 (The Wiring Guide) to 4,800,000 (styropyro). For E13 to E19 the multiple uses the study's 2026-10-24 count, as each file's header says.
