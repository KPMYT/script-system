## Why it works

The comments mostly argue the science, which is the engagement a settle-the-debate title buys. The most-liked comment corrects a point in the video: "The reason wire nuts don't have a current rating is because they are not intended to conduct electricity. They are intended hold the wires in intimate contact. The wires themselves are the bus." (3.9K likes), and two more make the same point about the resistance result (736 and 392 likes). Others add the benefit the criteria missed: "the main benefit of the WAGOs are that I can remove a single wire without needing to disconnect the others" (730 likes), and the mixed-wire failure drew a practical fix: "always run the stranded wire longer than the solid so the nut has something to grab onto" (90 likes). A professional sums up the reaction: "I bought a box of Wagos years ago and never went back" (712 likes).
