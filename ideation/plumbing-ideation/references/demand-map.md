## 4. Problem selection inside a proven template

How universal the problem is × how disgusting / annoying × how frequent × how expensive the pro fix. Universality sets the view ceiling. Once a template is proven, the work shifts to problem selection (the templated cleaning channel ran one skeleton with nouns swapped, 60k–553k).

## 5. Seasonal calendar (publish at the season's front edge)

- **Spring:** sump pumps and storm prep; sewer-root treatment (foam season); yard drainage; hose-bib first-use checks.
- **Summer:** outdoor spigots and sprinklers; drain smells worsening in heat; drain flies; vacation-prep checklists; water bills rising.
- **Fall:** winterization; the garden-hose disconnect rule; heat tape; insulating pipes; water-heater prep.
- **Winter:** frozen and burst pipes; water-heater failures and recovery; dripping-faucet freeze protection; ice-melt-era emergency content.
- **Evergreen anchors** (drains, toilets, water heaters, secrets listicles) carry the schedule between spikes.
