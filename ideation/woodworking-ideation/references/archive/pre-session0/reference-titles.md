## 1. The operator's own channel winners

Assigned to the **standard channel (Dale Oakfield's Workshop)** as the working assumption — reassign if the operator says otherwise. These are the only two of the operator's woodworking videos above 10k across many tests, so they are the highest-value data on what the operator's own audience will click.

- 10 AMAZING Woodworking Tips That Actually Work — 18k / 2 weeks
- 12 GENIUS Woodworking Tips Every Beginner Should Use — 14k / 4 days

**Lessons:** one format proven (F3 — numbered tips listicle + one caps power word + qualifying tail), zero specific topics proven ("woodworking tips" is a bucket, not a subject). "Every Beginner Should Use" confirms the audience skews **newer woodworker** — bias toward prohibition (F1), mistakes (F9), you're-doing-it-wrong (F6), and shortcut-secrets. The generic-tips format has a low ceiling (~18k); breaking past it means naming a **specific** high-demand topic and wrapping it in an exaggerated format. On the thumbnails, a sharp specific hook ("STOP GLUE STAINS") did the clicking while the title stayed broad — keep that pattern, and promote strong thumbnail hooks into their own dedicated titles.
