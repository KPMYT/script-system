## 5. Problem selection inside a proven template

Universality × how much it costs to get wrong (wasted wood, tools) × how aspirational the payoff × frequency. The beginner skew rewards "avoid this mistake" and "the skill that levels you up".

## 6. Seasonal calendar (publish at the season's front edge)

- **Q4 / holidays:** gift builds, projects that sell at craft fairs, "build these Christmas gifts from one board", ornaments — the high-volume season (proven: the Christmas-builds video).
- **Spring:** outdoor projects, garden furniture, planters, workshop setup / spring-clean the shop.
- **Summer:** beginner-project season (new hobbyists), deck and outdoor builds, drying and finishing (humidity affects finish and movement — a real angle).
- **Winter:** indoor shop projects, tool maintenance and sharpening, jig-building, tool-buying (post-holiday gift cards) — pairs with the high-monetization tool content.
- **Evergreen anchors** (joints, finishes, tips listicles, scrap / sawdust, tool-buying) carry the schedule between spikes.
- **For-profit seasonal additions (2026-09-09):** late June–early July — the wooden American flag and patriotic decor ("How to Make a Waving American Flag for $5 // HUGE PROFIT!" · 228k), thin-line first-responder variants; summer — planters and raised beds, outdoor chairs, wedding-season decor; September–October — fall planters (mums), fall editions ("6 More Woodworking Projects That Sell - Fall Edition" · 316k), craft-fair stock-building; November–December — the spiral tree ("One 2x4 Spiral Tree - Low Cost High Profit - Make Money Woodworking" · 1.0M), ornaments, reindeer, gift builds, the craft-fair peak (a third of the year's sales).
- **Operator rule (2026-06-22, confirmed 2026-09-09):** a seasonal title must match the publish calendar — a Fall title was killed in June for timing and became viable in September.
