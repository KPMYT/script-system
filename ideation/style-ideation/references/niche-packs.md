# Niche packs — style (for the `thumbnails` skill)

The `thumbnails` skill loads the named channel's pack as standing context. Fields: character description, common objects / materials / settings, banned items, style tweaks. **Status: DRAFT — every pack is `endorsed`-pending** (persona names, presenter type, disclosure and whether the presenter appears in the looks are open calls in `steering.md`). No niche thumbnail data yet — the layout section fills from the first screenshot batch.

## Layouts (proven on S1, the faceless clone channel, 2026-09-14 — 572k / 199k / 90k / 66k / 62k / 57k / 43k / 42k all run the same anatomy)

- **The same-presenter before / after split (proven)** — the generated presenter on both sides, the item the only variable (heavy black frames vs light tortoise, chunky beads vs a pendant, black turtleneck vs aqua, flat long hair vs a full bob), a neutral expression on the "before" and a smile on the "after", a red X and a green check in the corners on some; yellow + white caps hook of 2–4 lines centered, the number leading (5 GLASSES / 8 COLOR PAIRS / 6 WAYS), "AFTER 60" inside the hook, and the age scale in the thumbnail while the title says INSTANTLY (572k). The one non-split thumbnail (a title-case sentence hook over a garden shot) sat at the channel's known low (30k); the "10X YOUNGER" hyperbole didn't lift its title (≤30k). Never a real person's photos; no faces other than the presenter; consistent age across both panels.
- **The presenter + verdict** — the presenter on one third, the outfit or hairstyle on the other, a caps hook of 2–4 words: AGES YOU 10 YEARS · NOT BLACK · WEAR THIS · THE $0 TRICK · NEVER AFTER 60 · LOOKS CHEAP · 10 YEARS OFF · FRENCH WOMEN NEVER · THE FRENCH WAY · SO FRUMPY? · IT'S NOT YOUR WEIGHT · STOP THIS.
- **The red circle + arrow on the tell** — the cardigan hem, the shoe, the lip liner, the crown, the roots, the frame of the glasses.
- **The color drape** — swatches held beside a face (warm vs cool), the "washed out" vs "lit" pair; the interlock by withholding: the swatch blurred inside a red box ("NOT BLACK").
- **The item grid** — the numbered pieces (the capsule, the only-N-pieces) laid flat on a neutral ground; the plumbing contrast-listicle grid precedent (1.3M / 952k).
- **Cloned thumbnails lose for the second mover** — re-makes across the pair change the scene, not just the hook.
- **Brands:** never a logo, monogram or recognizable house design (the Chanel-style tweed silhouette, the Hermès-style print, the LV-style monogram are all out); "a tweed jacket", "a silk scarf", "a structured bag" are fine. Default off pending the portfolio call.

## Scarlett Sterling (ch. 1, the American affluent woman) — pack

- **Ecosystem model (S1):** the synthetic presenter is *shown wearing* the looks on both sides of every split — glasses, earrings, necklace, scarf, lipstick, color, hair. Whether Scarlett does the same is the operator's call (a demonstration by generated visuals; disclosure on); the seeds in `produced-log.md` assume she does.
- **Character:** American woman, 55–60, polished and expensive-looking without flash — a sharp bob or a smooth shoulder-length cut in silver-blonde, warm brunette or a deliberate silver (hair color TBC — fixed once), tailored blazer or a fine-knit crew, small gold or pearl earrings, a clean neutral palette (camel, cream, navy, ivory); understated real jewelry, never costume; manicured, minimal makeup with a defined lip. Expression: warm, knowing, the "let me tell you the truth" half-smile; gestures — the open palm toward the item, the raised finger for the one rule, the pointing verdict.
- **Wordmark:** none by default (a series key is open); the age-scale caps line ("10 YEARS OFF") is her recurring hook.
- **Common objects / settings:** a rack of neutral pieces; a blazer on a form; dark straight jeans beside a pull-on pair; a pointed flat beside an orthopedic sneaker; a silk scarf at a collar; a color drape by a face; a hairstyle pair (the set-and-sprayed short cut vs the soft bob); a foundation bottle and a cream compact; a pair of glasses that lift vs frames that droop; a pearl earring catching light. Settings: a bright, quiet dressing room or living room with white walls and one good chair; a boutique interior; a tree-lined street; never a logo wall.
- **Banned items:** logos, monograms and recognizable house designs; lingerie, swimwear crops or any suggestive framing; body-shaming imagery (no "before" bodies — only "before" garments); real people's photos and celebrities; medical imagery (needles, procedures); diet imagery (scales, tape measures); faces other than the presenter; text over hands.
- **Style tweaks:** clean, luminous, expensive — soft daylight, cream and camel grounds, one accent color; the before / after where only the piece changes; the red X on the garment, never on a person; the numbered item grid for capsule titles; peak moment is the presenter placing the third piece or the scarf.

## The French persona (ch. 2) — pack

- **Character:** French woman, 55–65, effortless — natural hair worn loose or in a soft low chignon (gray-streaked dark or a deliberate silver — fixed once), a Breton-striped top or a trench, a silk scarf, minimal jewelry, a bare face with a red or berry lip, low-key confidence. Expression: dry, amused, the raised eyebrow and the small shrug; gestures — the two-finger "less", the hand on the scarf, the flat palm "stop".
- **Wordmark:** THE FRENCH WAY top-left on every series title (endorsed-pending); FRENCH WOMEN NEVER as the contrast-pair hook.
- **Common objects / settings:** the trench on a hook; the Breton top; a scarf tied at the neck; ballet flats or loafers on a herringbone floor; a small edited rail of neutrals beside an overflowing closet (the contrast pair); a red lipstick; a pharmacy bottle without a label (micellar water, thermal spray); air-dried hair beside a set-and-sprayed style; a café table, a balcony rail, a Haussmann-style façade, a market basket. Settings: a Paris apartment with tall windows and a marble mantel, a café terrace, a quiet street — **at most one Paris cue per frame**, never the cliché stack (no beret + baguette + Eiffel Tower together).
- **Banned items:** the costume clichés (beret, baguette, striped mime look, the Eiffel Tower as the whole frame); logos and house designs; suggestive framing; diet or medical imagery; real people and celebrities; "American women" depicted as a person to mock — the contrast side is always a garment, a closet or a habit, never a woman; faces other than the presenter; text over hands.
- **Style tweaks:** restrained — muted stone, navy and cream grounds, natural light, one red accent (the lip, the scarf); the "less vs more" split (the edited rail vs the crammed closet; the bare face vs the heavy face); the item grid for the only-N-pieces titles; peak moment is the scarf tie or the shrug.
