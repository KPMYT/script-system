## 3. Shared rules for the two-channel set (one topic pool, two lenses)

Ported from the woodworking Dale ↔ Hank pair rules and the home-security three-channel rules:

- **One pool, two lenses.** Every topic in `demand-map.md` may run on both channels: ch. 1 as the wealthy insider's verdict, ch. 2 as the French practice. Natural owners where the lens is unequal: color analysis, the "look expensive" lane, glasses, bras and shapewear-as-proportion, makeup texture, the American occasion lane (weddings, cruises, holidays) → **ch. 1**; the capsule and the uniform, the scarf and the trench, gray on purpose, air-dried hair, the bare face and the red lip, the pharmacy, the anti-excess contrarians → **ch. 2**. Apex topics (what ages you, hair after 60, colors near the face, the pieces every woman needs) run on **both** with the format-flip.
- **Format-flip:** where one channel runs the listicle, the other runs the single on the same topic (and vice versa); different counts, different power-adjectives, different anchors (ch. 1: expensive / elegant / 10 years; ch. 2: chic / rules / the French way).
- **Collision audit before every lock:** list every same-topic pair across the two batches; **strong twins** (same structure, near-identical wording) are split — one topic per channel, the other drops it; light overlaps are differentiated by number, lens phrase or anchor.
- **Ruling 2026-09-14:** the operator may run one topic set on both channels as intentional twins (batch 1 did — 25 for 25); the strong-twin split is then waived, the twins are logged as variants in both produced logs, and the differentiation moves to the lens words, the counts and the thumbnails.
- **Re-makes across the pair are intentional variants**, flagged in `produced-log.md`; the ecosystem clones winners across channels and it works, but the second mover on a cloned thumbnail lost 7× in the home-security head-to-head — the thumbnails differ even when the topic repeats (`niche-packs.md`).
- **Collab tagging:** if one channel becomes the flagship, tag the sibling's uploads with it (core `framework.md` §11) and record every credit in the intake record.
- **Shelf lanes are per channel** (etiquette on ch. 1, art de vivre on ch. 2), run only on the operator's call, never enter `demand-map.md`, and open the craft-section question only at 1.5× the channel's normal.
